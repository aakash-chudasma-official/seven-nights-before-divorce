# Chapter 8 — Night Six: The Apologies That Came Too Late

Tuesday four baje Meera ke internet ne kaam kiya.

Usne call se pehle router restart kiya tha, phone charge kiya tha, notebook kholi thi. Current coordinator ka naam Farah tha. Usne opening mein kaha, "Main clear kar doon, hum bookshop nahi khol rahe."

Meera ne kaha, "Haan. Main bhi abhi shop propose nahi kar rahi."

Farah ne chaar Sunday sessions ki jagah pehle ek trial session suggest kiya. Adults ke liye, mixed reading levels. Registration library karegi. Meera selection aur discussion plan bheje. Honorarium modest tha, travel se thoda zyada. Usne amount likha. Phir poocha preparation ka expected scope kya hai.

Farah kuch seconds chup hui. Meera ko laga sawal galat tha. Phir usne detailed answer diya. "Good you asked," kaha. Meera ne pen se us line ke saamne ek dot laga diya. Usse call ke baad yaad rakhna tha ki amount poochne se duniya khatam nahi hui.

Possible date September mein thi. Approval pending. Call twenty-two minutes chali. Meera ne thank you kaha, phir phone band karte hi apni notebook dekhne lagi. Usne itni preparation ki thi, aur abhi usse syllabus bhi nahi banana tha. Bas do-page proposal.

Usne Naina ko voice note bheja. Background mein pressure cooker ki awaaz thi. Naina ne reply kiya: proud, but proposal free mein hundred pages mat bana dena.

Meera hansi. Phir usne retainer ka pending payment check kiya. Nahi aaya tha. Usne reminder bheja aur September session ke liye alag document khola.

Six-ten par carpenter aaya. Aarav abhi lift mein tha. Aadmi ne table ko ulta kiya, bracket hilaaya aur bola, "Screw thread gaya. Sirf tight nahi hoga."

Meera ne haan kaha. Aarav aaya toh usne use explain kiya. Aadmi ne washers nikaale, ek replacement bracket dikhaya, price bataya. Aarav ne approve kiya. Meera kitchen mein paani laayi. Carpenter ne room ki taraf dekh kar poocha shifting hai kya. Usne kaha haan. Aadmi ne kaha rain cover double lagwana. Phir table fix karne laga.

Seven baje table seedhi khadi thi.

Aarav ne palm se corner press kiya. Hili nahi. Carpenter ne doosre corner par pressure diya. Woh bhi theek. Meera ne invoice ka photo liya. Aarav ne payment ki, thoda sa sawal labour aur material ke breakup par, phir done.

Aadmi chala gaya. Aarav ne table par paani ka glass rakha.

"Kaafi din ka kaam tha," usne kaha.

Meera ne eyebrow uthaya.

"Matlab pending," usne add kiya.

"Haan."

Woh glass ka reflection dekh rahi thi. Table ab bhi scratches wali thi. Corner ka chipped polish ab bhi chipped. Fix hone se nayi nahi ho gayi. Usne cloth uthaya aur carpenter ke sawdust ko table se hata diya.

Documents sort karte waqt yellow envelope uske haath aaya.

Inside card thi. Bicycle par flowers ka illustration. Meera ne pehle envelope ke peeche dekha. Koi date nahi. Aarav ki handwriting mein sirf M tha, phir ek attempted flower jo sun jaisa lagta tha.

Usne card khola.

*Tumhare paas ghar aakar boring baat bhi interesting lagti hai. Birthday par toh kuch achha bolna chahiye, par abhi yehi sach hai. Sunday ko kahin chalte hain. Phones silent. — A.*

Woh chair par baith gayi.

Card ki fold ke paas paper soft ho gayi thi. Usne ek baar pehle bhi padhi thi, February 2023 mein, flowers ke saath. Tab usne thank you kaha tha, aur card drawer mein rakhi thi. Us waqt uska throat itna tight tha ki words ka meaning accept karna mushkil tha.

Kitchen se Aarav ne poocha, "Dal garam karoon?"

Meera ne jawab dene se pehle card band ki.

"Haan."

Usne envelope table par rakha. Woh visible reh gayi.

***

Aarav ne card ko door se pehchaan liya.

Usne bicycle wali cards us bookstore se li thi jahan Meera kabhi lunch break mein jaati thi. Owner ne offer diya tha: four for three. Aarav ne four liye. Ek Kabir ki daughter ke liye, ek parents ke anniversary ke liye. Do blank. Yeh blank card thi. Usne Meera ke birthday ke ek din pehle likhi thi, office se lautne ke baad. Woh living room mein author ka call le rahi thi. Aarav ne bedroom mein baith kar likha, phir envelope drawer mein rakh di, kyunki subah dena tha.

Subah warehouse se call aaya. Staff ki galti aur software mismatch dono the. Usne breakfast ke beech kaam shuru kiya. Meera ne kaha shaam ko seven-thirty table hai. Usne haan kaha, uske head par kiss ki, aur nikla. Card drawer mein rahi.

Raat ki chronology use bahut clearly yaad thi. Seven-twelve par usne likha leaving in ten. Seven-forty par truck details correct nahi thi. Eight-ten par Meera ka call. Usne reject karke message kiya on call. Eight-forty par boss se discussion. Nine par problem technically solved thi. Lekin paperwork baaki tha, aur Aarav ko ab us restaurant mein jaakar uske saamne baithne se darr lag raha tha.

Usne kaam ko aur thoda stretch kiya. Yehi part usne kabhi accept nahi kiya tha.

Actual delay tha. Avoidance bhi thi.

Woh eleven ke baad ghar aaya. Meera bedroom mein thi. Table par restaurant ka paper bag rakha tha. Usne kaha sorry, absolute mess tha. Meera ne poocha pehle kyun nahi bataya. Usne warehouse ke details bataye. Usne kaha main wait kar rahi thi. Aarav ne kaha I said sorry na.

Meera ne uski taraf dekha aur bathroom mein chali gayi.

Usse laga tha ab drama hoga. Uske iss thought par ab usse sharm aayi. Woh adult woman thi jo birthday dinner mein akele baithi thi. Uske face ko drama bolkar usne khud ko easier role de diya tha: exhausted man dealing with unreasonable reaction.

Do din baad flowers liye. Card drawer se nikaali. Weekend plan repeat kiya. Meera ne Sunday ko deadline bol di. Usne turant kaha theek, phir kabhi. Relief bhi hua tha.

Aaj usne dal ki gas low ki. Living room mein aaya.

"Birthday wali," usne kaha.

Meera ne card par haath rakha. "Haan."

Usne chair kheench kar baithna chaha, phir poocha, "Baat karein?"

"Abhi?"

"Nahi karni toh—"

"Baitho."

Woh baitha. Table nahi hili. Is ordinary improvement se usse aur awkward laga.

"Mujhe woh night yaad hai," usne kaha.

Meera ne halka sa sir hilaaya. "Mujhe bhi."

"Maine kaha tha leaving. Jab pata tha nahi nikal raha."

Uski aankhein uthi. Aarav apne haath dekhne laga.

"Pehle laga bas ten minutes. Phir... phir bataane mein darr lag raha tha. Main aur late hota gaya."

Meera ne kaafi der kuch nahi kaha. Usne socha shayad woh bolegi ab kya karoon is information ka. Woh reasonable sawal hota.

Usne kaha, "Main waiter ko bolti rahi aap order hold karo."

Aarav ne uski taraf dekha.

"Pehle mujhe laga late ho. Phir laga accident. Phir online dikhe. Main itni stupid feel kar rahi thi ki relief bhi properly nahi hua."

"Sorry."

Word chhota laga. Usne add kiya, "Mujhe call karna tha. Sach batana tha ki nahi pata kab nikloonga."

"Haan."

"Aur ghar aake tumhe bolne dena tha."

Meera ne card ka corner dabaya. "Us din se pehle bhi aise hua tha. Isliye birthday zyada yaad raha. Special day tha, par bas birthday nahi tha."

"Pata hai."

Usne turant kaha, phir correct kiya. "Ab sun raha hoon. Tab nahi pata tha."

Meera ne apna face side kiya. Usne tissue lene ka socha. Phir usse laga uska har gesture conversation interrupt karega. Tissue usi table ke corner par tha. Woh khud le sakti thi. Agar poochna ho toh pooch sakta tha. Yeh bhi itna calculate kyun kar raha tha?

"Paani doon?" usne aakhir kaha.

"Haan."

Usne glass bhara. Meera ne liya aur half piya. Uski nose red thi. Aarav ko woh purani photo yaad nahi aayi. Uske saamne abhi ka face tha, tired aur angry aur familiar.

"Main kabhi-kabhi birthday har argument mein laati thi," usne kaha. "Woh bhi..."

"Abhi uski baat mat karo," Aarav ne kaha.

Meera ne dekha.

Usne dheere kaha, "Main sorry bol raha hoon. Tumhe abhi apna fault add nahi karna."

Woh kuch seconds use dekhti rahi. Phir card fold karke rakh di. "Theek."

Kitchen mein dal thodi zyada boil hui. Lid ke paas foam nikli. Aarav utha, gas band ki, stove pochha. Meera kitchen tak aayi, phir door par ruk gayi.

"Sunday ko main free thi," usne kaha.

Woh uski taraf muda.

"Card mein jo likha. Main free thi. Maine deadline bola tha kyunki... mujhe gift lena ya outing karna us din achha nahi lag raha tha. Aur tumne phir kabhi poocha nahi."

Aarav ne cloth sink mein rakha. "Mujhe laga tum nahi chahti."

"Main chahti thi tum phir poocho."

Uska pehla reaction tha, mujhe kaise pata chalta. Usne woh sentence nahi bola. Yeh uska ek familiar loop tha. Woh pooch sakta tha; Meera bol sakti thi. Lekin ab woh birthday ki uski galti ko communication puzzle bana kar solve nahi karna chahta tha.

"Maine phir nahi poocha," usne kaha.

Meera ne haan ki. "Ab bhi... mujhe nahi pata kya chahiye sun kar. Bas tumhe pata rahe."

"Haan."

***

Dinner ke baad Meera ne card books ke box mein nahi rakhi. Usne personal papers ka small pouch khola. Wahaan old tickets, Papa ka ek handwritten shopping list aur ek broken earring thi. Card usme fit ho gayi. Envelope thoda bahar reh gaya; usne zor se push nahi kiya.

Aarav apne Papa ko knee exercises ka video call kar raha tha. Papa ne phone ko itna neeche rakha ki sirf ceiling dikh rahi thi. Aarav bol raha tha, "Papa, camera knee ki taraf. Nahi, phone mat girao."

Meera ko hansi aayi. Phir Sunanda ka hospital room yaad aa gaya.

May 2025. Nashik. Uske paas room ki photo thi, Aarav ne bheji thi. Ma side mein baithi thi, green bedsheet, tray par steel glass. Condition serious nahi thi, lekin admission unexpected tha. Aarav ne call kiya toh Meera ek author ke chapter par kaam kar rahi thi. Deadline real thi. Author third reminder bhej chuka tha.

Us week unki ladai bhi hui thi. Kisi family lunch par Aarav ne sabke saamne kaha tha Meera ghar se kaam karti hai, flexible hai. Usne baad mein bataya use bura laga. Aarav ne kaha tha flexible hona insult kaise hai? Woh do din se short replies de rahi thi.

Hospital ka call aaya toh woh poochna chahti thi, kya main aaoon? Par uske andar ek chhota, ugly thought tha: ab meri flexibility chahiye hogi.

Us thought ko usne deadline ke neeche chhupa diya.

Aarav ne kaha tha, don't come, main manage kar loonga. Usne turant okay kaha. Baad mein Naina ko bola tha Aarav ne mana kiya. Woh statement technically sahi thi. Phone ke pehle half ki baat usne nahi batayi thi, jahaan usne kaha tha this week is impossible.

Ab usne pouch band kiya. Video call khatam hone ka wait kiya. Aarav living room mein aaya toh woh sofa par baithi thi.

"Tum busy ho?" usne poocha.

"Nahi."

"Nashik wali baat kehni hai. Ma admit thi jab."

Uska face thoda band hua. Meera ko laga woh kahega ab kyun. Usne nahi kaha. Chair par baith gaya.

"Maine deadline ka bola tha. Woh thi. Par main aa sakti thi. Ek din ke liye. Author se extension pooch sakti thi."

Aarav ne kaha, "Maine mana kiya tha."

"Usse pehle main mana kar rahi thi. Direct nahi. Tumhe bolne de rahi thi ki mat aao."

Usne jaw move kiya, jaise kuch dabaya ho.

Meera ne apni fingers ek saath ki. "Mujhe tum par gussa tha. Lunch wali baat. Ghar se kaam, flexible. Uska gussa Ma ke hospital mein le gayi. Sorry."

Room mein kaafi der koi awaaz nahi thi. Aarav ne cup uthaya, phir bina piye rakh diya.

"Mujhe laga tha," usne kaha.

Meera ne sir uthaaya.

"Ki tum gusse mein ho. Par maine poocha nahi. Main bhi chahta tha tum khud bolo aa rahi ho."

"Haan."

"Hospital mein Ma pooch rahi thi kaam bahut hai kya. Maine bola haan."

Usne aankhein neeche kar li. Yeh usse nahi pata tha.

"Unhe kya boloon," Aarav ne kaha. "Ki hum..."

Woh sentence chhod gaya. Meera ne use complete nahi kiya.

"Mujhe unse bhi sorry bolna hai," usne kaha.

Aarav ne seedha dekha. "Mera part pehle rehne do. Har cheez sabko explain nahi karni."

Meera ko uski tone se chot lagi. Phir usne samjha woh apne hurt ko kisi aur call mein lose nahi karna chahta tha.

"Theek hai," usne kaha.

Aarav khada hua. "Main walk par jaa raha hoon. Neeche."

Usne haan kaha. Woh shoes pehenne laga. Is baar koi thirty-minute promise nahi tha. Meera ne clock nahi dekhi. Door band hone ke baad usne apna pouch lap mein rakha aur sofa par baithi rahi.

Usse laga tha confession ke baad halka feel hoga. Nahi hua. Aarav ko woh sach jaanna zaroori tha, lekin sach bolne ka immediate reward nahi tha.

Twenty minutes baad Naina ka call aaya. Usne nahi uthaya. Message kiya: aaj nahi, kal?

Reply mein okay aur ek photo aayi. Vihaan ka volcano finally erupt hua tha, sink mein. Red foam floor tak thi. Meera ne photo zoom ki. Uske muh par smile aayi, phir gayi. Usne heart bheja. Apni life ki saari baatein ek hi person ko ek hi raat batana zaroori nahi tha.

Aarav thirty-five minutes baad aaya. Grocery bag mein doodh tha. Usne fridge mein rakha. Meera ne poocha coffee? Usne kaha raat ho gayi. Usse instantly rejection jaisa feel hua. Phir usne dekha clock ten-forty-five thi.

"Haan," usne kaha. "Paani?"

"Le liya."

Woh guest room ki taraf gaya. Door par ruk kar bola, "Main us baat par abhi okay nahi hoon."

Meera ka throat tight hua. "Pata hai."

"Par suna. Jo bola."

"Haan."

Woh andar chala gaya. Meera ne card wala pouch apne bag mein rakha. Zip band ki. Table par carpenter ki receipt reh gayi thi. Usne Aarav ke bills folder ke paas rakh di. Uske baad lights band karne lagi.

Guest room ke door ke neeche light thi. Usne knock nahi kiya.
