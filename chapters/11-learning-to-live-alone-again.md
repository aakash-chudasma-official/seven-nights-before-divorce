# Chapter 11 — Learning to Live Alone Again

February ki subah Meera ke client ne retainer renew nahi kiya.

Mail polite thi. Budget review. Great working with you. Keep in touch. Usne last sentence do baar padha. Phir invoice folder khola. Previous month ka payment pending tha. Renewal ke bina bhi woh payment due thi. Usne reply mein thanks ke saath invoice ka reminder daala.

Send karne ke baad uske haath thande ho gaye.

Window ke paas green curtain thi. Ma ne jo bheji thi, length six inches zyada thi. Meera ne fold karke clips lagayi thi. Har baar wash ke baad fold dobara banana padta. Naina kehti tailor se shorten karwa lo. Meera kehti next week. Six months mein kuch next weeks aise hi rahe.

Usne budget khola. Rent. Electricity. Internet. Groceries. Ma ke liye monthly small transfer. Woh transfer essential category mein tha; Ma bolti thi mat bhejo, phir secretly usi amount ka koi dabba courier karti. Meera ne numbers dekhe. Buffer ab bhi tha, lekin income ka stable part gaya tha.

Pehla impulse Aarav ko message karne ka hua. Financial problem ko uske saamne rakhne se woh shape mein aa jaati thi. Woh alternatives bata deta. Kabhi dismissive, kabhi genuinely useful. Us aadat ko usne six months mein erase nahi kiya tha.

Usne phone khola. Aarav ka last message ek document scan tha, ten days pehle. Address update ke liye. Meera ne chat close ki aur Naina ko call kiya.

"Kaam gaya," usne kaha.

Naina ne pehle kaha, "Kaunsa?"

Meera ne bataya. Naina office lift mein thi. Signal cut ho gaya. Call disconnected. Do minutes baad wapas aaya.

"Sorry. Tu ro rahi?"

"Nahi. Abhi nahi."

"Okay. Bata."

Meera ne details batayi. Phir clear kaha, "Referrals chahiye. Aur rates negotiate karne mein help. Sympathy bhi chalegi, par kaam pehle."

Naina ne dry laugh ki. "Sympathy ka budget bhi kam hai. Deadline hai. Par do contacts hain. Shaam ko bhejoongi."

"Aaj shaam?"

"Haan. Tu reminder daal dena. Main bhool sakti hoon."

Meera ne kaha okay. Usse reminder dena ab bhi irritating lag sakta tha. Lekin friend ne pehle se kaha tha. Woh apni need aur doosre ki capacity ko ek hi cheez nahi samajhna chahti thi.

Call ke baad usne library session ka poster khola. Agla session Sunday ko tha. Six participants registered. Honorarium se rent nahi aata tha. Aur har session ke pehle use doubt hota tha ki log kyun sunenge. September ka pehla trial uneven gaya tha. Do people late aaye. Ek participant poore time author ko wrong prove karta raha. Meera ne apna elaborate discussion plan complete karne ki koshish ki, jis se session rushed laga. Farah ne baad mein kaha, next time fewer questions.

Usne next time half plan banaya. Phir bhi two hours preparation hui. Yeh koi overnight success nahi thi. Lekin third session mein ek woman apni copy mein sticky notes lekar aayi thi. Usne Meera se poocha next reading kya hai. Meera ne us raat khud ko cafe se decent coffee pilaayi thi. Ghar aakar invoice reminder bhi bheja tha. Dono ek hi din hue the.

Aaj usne Sunday ka excerpt print kiya. Printer ab uske paas nahi tha; corner shop jaana padta. Small inconvenience. Kabhi yeh walk din ka ek hi outside trip hoti. Aaj usne file phone mein daali aur keys li.

Neeche shop par machine jam thi. Aadmi ne kaha ten minutes. Meera ne next shop jaane ki jagah wait kiya. Usne stationery shelf se rubber bands liye. Glass jar mein orange-handle scissors thi. Uske paas already thi. Usne haath nahi badhaya.

Print aaya toh ek page light tha. Usne reprint maanga. Aadmi ne kaha readable hai. Meera ne page wapas diya. "Mujhe dark chahiye. Participants ko copy deni hai."

Usne reprint kar diya. Koi fight nahi hui. Meera ne paise diye aur dhoop mein bahar aayi. Aaj ke sab problems is tarah solve nahi hone wale the. Phir bhi ek page theek hua tha.

***

Aarav Friday ko office se seven-fifteen par nikla. Gate tak pahunchkar laptop ki taraf haath gaya. Ek mail ka reply baaki tha. Reply na ho toh shipment rukti nahi. Subah tak ho sakta tha. Usne bag back seat par rakha aur car start ki.

Twenty minutes baad phone ring hua. Boss.

"Need a quick revision."

Aarav ne car side mein ki. Details suni. Client Monday morning tak deck chahta tha. Boss ko Sunday evening dekhna tha. Aarav ne kaha Saturday ko ek hour de sakta hoon, Sunday available nahi.

"Family?" boss ne poocha.

"Personal plan."

"Okay, but keep phone on."

Usne haan kaha. Boundary half thi. Usse pata tha Sunday ko phone check karega. Lekin pehle woh Saturday aur Sunday dono de deta. Is baar usne one hour calendar mein block kiya aur remaining work Kabir ke saath divide kiya. Kabir ne kaha mujhe Saturday school ka event hai. Aarav ko irritation hui, phir unhone Monday early slot dekha. Deck ban sakti thi. Har person available nahi tha, iska matlab koi irresponsible nahi tha.

November mein usne Aundh flat chhod diya tha. Lease renew karna afford ho sakta tha, lekin unnecessary bada tha. Naya one-bedroom Baner ke doosre end par tha. Rent kam, commute thoda better, kitchen smaller. Dining table mushkil se fit hui. Usne chair wall se laga di. Fixed bracket ne move survive kiya. Ek shelf panel chipped hua. Landlord ne inspection ke time deposit mein cleaning charge kaat li. Aarav ne receipt maangi, negotiate kiya, thoda recover hua. Usne Meera ko uske share ka adjusted amount transfer kiya aur calculation sheet bheji.

Meera ne ek line item query ki. Uska pehla response defensive draft tha: I have already paid. Usne bheja nahi. Receipt check ki. Same expense do baar count ho gaya tha. Usne correct amount bheja. Meera ne thanks kaha. Usne corrected receipt folder mein rakhi aur sheet close ki.

Naye flat mein guitar wall ke against thi. Repair September mein karayi thi. String change, basic servicing. Shop wale ne kaha fingers hurt hongi, years baad. Hui bhi. Aarav ne teen nights practice ki, phir ten days nahi. Phir Saturday nikaali. Ab week mein do baar karta, agar yaad rahe. Same opening chords. Kuch naya seekha bhi tha, lekin video pause kiye bina nahi.

Kabir ghar aaya toh bola play. Aarav ne kaha abhi nahi. Kabir ne force nahi kiya. Dono ne tea pi, daughter ke annual day ki shaky video dekhi. Kabir ne beech mein poocha Meera se baat hoti? Aarav ne kaha mostly documents. Kabhi library updates. Kabir ne head hilaaya. Phir apartment ke parking rules ki complaint shuru kar di. Aarav ko achha laga friend uski shaadi ko har visit ka main subject nahi banata.

Us raat Aarav ne laundry machine chalayi aur detergent zyada daal diya. Foam kaafi thi. Usne rinse repeat kiya. Eleven baje bhi kapde wet the. Usne drying stand lagaya. Meera hoti toh kya kehti, yeh thought aaya. Shayad kuch nahi. Shayad same instruction jo packet par thi. Usne next time ke liye cap par marker se line banayi.

Saturday morning revision hour ninety minutes gaya. Usne irritate hokar Kabir ko message kiya ki time underestimate hua. Kabir ne likha welcome to our industry. Aarav ko joke bura laga, phir lunch ke baad theek. Usne laptop band kiya aur Nashik jaane ka bag pack kiya.

Sunday Papa ka birthday nahi tha. Koi appointment bhi nahi. Usne bas visit plan ki thi. Ma ne pehle poocha special kya. Usne kaha kuch nahi. Unhe believe karne mein time laga.

Nashik mein Papa ne usse car insurance ki renewal yaad dilaayi. Aarav ne kaha January mein ho gayi. Papa ne haan kaha, phir do minutes baad policy ka copy maanga. Aarav ne forward ki. Gussa aane wala tha; phir usne poocha aapka renewal kab hai? Papa ne file nikaali. Dono dining table par dates dekhne lage.

Ma kitchen se boli, "Aaya toh file lekar baith gaye. Bahar chale jao thoda."

Aarav hansa. Papa ne file close nahi ki. Usne khud folder band karke kaha, "Walk?"

Knee ab better thi, perfect nahi. Papa slow chale. Corner ki pharmacy par ruke, kyunki aadmi ko unse kuch poochna tha. Aarav neeche step par wait karta raha. Usse phone check karne ka urge aaya. Usne ek baar check kiya. Boss ka mail tha. Usne Monday reply ka flag lagaya. Pocket mein rakh diya.

Walk par Papa ne Meera ka naam liya. Pehle library ka poocha. Aarav ne kaha monthly sessions karti hai. Papa ne poocha paise milte? Uske andar old irritation uthi. Usne kaha thode. Papa ne haan kaha. Phir bole, "Achha hai. Usse books pasand thi."

"Hain," Aarav ne kaha.

Papa ne use dekha. "Haan. Hain."

Thodi der woh road ke broken patch se bach kar chale. Papa ne koi advice nahi di. Corner par rukkar unhone kaha, "Tujhe bura lagta hoga."

Aarav ne footpath ki taraf dekha. "Kabhi bahut."

"Haan."

Bas itna. Aarav ko ek second ko laga aur kuch bolenge. Papa ne pocket se tissue nikala, nose pochhi aur poocha chai yahin lein? Aarav ne haan kaha.

Chai par Ma ka phone aaya, dinner ke liye late mat karna. Papa ne kaha aa rahe. Phir Aarav ko bataya ki Sunanda ne ab Sunday drawing class join ki hai. Aarav ko pata nahi tha. Usne poocha kab se. Papa ne kaha two months. Usse thoda shame feel hua. Woh apni problems mein Ma ki life ko sirf concern calls samajhta raha tha.

Ghar jaakar usne Ma se drawings maangi. Unhone pehle kaha kya dekhna, basic hai. Usne insist kiya. Notebook mein flowers, ek cup, aur chair ka sketch tha. Perspective crooked thi. Aarav ne kaha colour achha hai. Ma ne poocha sach? Usne haan kaha. Papa ne kitchen se kaha frame karwa lete hain. Ma hansi aur boli pehle achhi bana loon.

Aarav ne notebook wapas di. Usne photo nahi li. Har affection ko shareable proof nahi banana tha. Woh wahin baitha aur next page par Ma ko outline complete karte dekhta raha.

***

Sunday session mein six mein se four participants aaye.

Meera ne chairs circle mein nahi lagayi. Reading table par copies rakhi, paani ki bottle, aur three questions apne notebook mein. Ek participant ne kaha usne reading nahi ki. Meera ne kaha first paragraph yahin padh lete hain. Usne apni careful introduction ka half chhod diya.

Discussion shuru mein awkward thi. Ek young man har question par kehta depends. Meera ko irritation hui. Pehle woh usse correct karne lagti. Aaj usne poocha kis cheez par depends? Usne pehli baar example diya. Baaki woman ne disagree kiya. Uske baad unhe bolne ke liye push nahi karna pada.

End par Farah ne next month ka slot poocha. Meera ne tentative yes kaha aur honorarium release date confirm ki. Phir copies gather ki. Table par ek page reh gaya tha. Us par kisi ne character ke sentence ke neeche likha tha: not sure I agree.

Meera ne page photo nahi ki. File mein rakh liya. Usse achha laga ki room mein kisi ne agree karna duty nahi samjha.

Ghar lautkar usne fridge khola. Leftovers nahi the. Naina ne referrals bheje the. Ek publisher interested tha, lekin test edit unpaid. Meera ne sample length poochi. Do thousand words. Usne kaha shorter sample ya paid test possible? Reply abhi nahi aaya.

Uske baad loneliness aayi. Session ke baad expected high nahi. Sunday afternoon, ghar quiet, lunch banane ka mann nahi. Usne toast nikala, phir app kholi. Delivery charge dekhkar band ki. Phir khud par gussa aaya. Agar monthly budget allow karta tha toh ek meal order kar sakti thi. Usne dal-rice bowl order kiya. Delivery time thirty-five minutes.

Wait karte waqt Aarav ka status dekha. Nashik ki road ki photo thi, car passenger seat se. Woh drive nahi kar raha tha, shayad Papa. Usne photo ko zoom nahi kiya. Phir socha Papa driving kar sakte hain toh knee better hogi. Usne message kiya:

> Papa ka knee ab kaisa hai?

Reply thodi der baad aaya:

> Better. Walk ki aaj. Tumhara session tha?

Usne haan kaha. Four people. Good discussion. Aarav ne poocha excerpt kaunsa. Usne title bheja. Woh book uske paas nahi thi. Usne library se li thi.

Chat wahin ruk gayi. Meera ko uske reply ka wait karna pada. Ten minutes. Phir usne phone rakh diya. Aarav ne har shared title par immediate response dene ka promise nahi kiya tha. Woh naye boundaries ko samajh sakti thi aur phir bhi unse lonely feel kar sakti thi.

Food aaya. Usne table par baithkar khaya. Bowl photo mein bada tha, actual mein small. Taste achha tha. Usne review mein portion small likha. Phir app band ki. Usne fridge se dahi nikala aur bacha hua bowl uske saath khaya.

Shaam ko Ma ki video call hui. Ma ne curtain dekhi aur kaha abhi tak shorten nahi karayi? Meera ne kaha nahi. Ma boli courier kar do main karwa doon. Usne kaha nahi, local tailor hai, le jaoongi. Phir khud hansi. Next week.

Ma ne birthday ka poocha. Twenty-first ko Meera thirty-four ki hogi. Dinner ghar par ya bahar? Woh chhoti si choice uske liye suddenly difficult ho gayi. Old birthday yaad aaya. Usne saans li aur kaha bahar, lekin afternoon. Ma ke saath, Naina agar free ho. Usne actual plan phone par banaya. Reservation woh karegi. Kisi ko guess nahi karna tha.

Call ke baad Aarav ka message aaya. Usne excerpt ka first paragraph read kiya tha; online sample mila. Ek line usse interesting lagi. Usne woh quote nahi bheja, bas poocha tumne participants se narrator par trust ka discuss kiya?

Meera ne phone apne paas rakha. Woh reply kar sakti thi. Kal bhi. Abhi uska proposal revise karna tha aur ek friend ne movie ka poocha tha. Usne Aarav ko likha, haan, kal bataoongi, aaj thak gayi. Usne okay bheja.

Woh reply dekhkar usne phone side mein rakha aur laptop par movie kholi. Twenty minutes baad so gayi. End next day dekhna tha.

Monday ko test edit wale publisher ne call kiya. Meera ne sample length par jo query bheji thi, woh uske saamne khuli thi.

"Hum normally paid test nahi karte," commissioning editor ne kaha. "Sab freelancers same process se aate."

Meera ne laptop par sample dekha. Dense academic text thi. Two thousand words ka clean edit half-hour ka kaam nahi tha. Author references bhi check karne the.

"Main five hundred words kar sakti hoon," usne kaha. "Style assess karne ke liye enough hona chahiye. Full sample paid hoga toh kar loongi."

Udhar chair ki awaaz aayi. Editor ne kaha budget tight hai. Meera ko turant apna rent yaad aaya. Woh haan keh sakti thi. Sample kar leti. Agar assignment milta toh compensate ho jaata. Agar nahi milta toh bhi experience. Uske dimaag mein saare familiar sentences ready the.

"Rate jo aapne bheja," usne kaha, "usme reference verification include nahi kar paoongi. Language edit kar sakti hoon."

Editor ne poocha references ka alag kitna. Meera ne apni sheet kholi. Quote diya. Apni awaaz sunte hue usse lag raha tha bahut zyada bol diya. Sheet mein wahi number tha jo usne carefully calculate kiya tha. Usne sentence ke end mein negotiable add nahi kiya.

Editor ne kaha check karke batati hoon. Call band hui. Meera five minutes screen dekhti rahi. Phir Naina ko message kiya: shayad project gaya.

Naina ne call kiya. "Kya hua?"

Meera ne bataya. Friend ne pehle kaha, "Achha kiya." Phir poocha budget manage hoga? Meera ko yeh sawal chubha. Use sirf applause chahiye tha, abhi. Phir usne apni sheet kholi.

"Two months," usne kaha. "Agar next payment aaye."

Naina chup hui. Phir boli, "Toh lower rate ka chhota job lena ho toh le lena. Is ek call ko apni full identity mat bana."

Meera ko advice poori pasand nahi aayi. Usne haan kaha, lekin dry tone mein. Call ke baad usne socha friend ko money stress ka answer expected kaise ho. Usne help maangi thi. Friend ne concern dikhaya. Har concern uski ability par doubt nahi tha.

Do ghante baad publisher ka mail aaya. References kisi aur ko denge. Five hundred-word test acceptable tha. Rate uske quote se thoda kam. Meera ne hours calculate kiye. Workable tha, agar scope written rahe. Usne acceptance mein boundaries clear likhi aur sample deadline Tuesday evening rakhi.

Lunch mein usne kal ka rice garam kiya, aur first paragraph mein ek sentence par twenty minutes atak gayi. Text samajh nahi aa raha tha. Usne query mark ki.

Evening tak sample ready hua. Send karne se pehle usne ek typo pakda jo usne khud introduce kiya tha. Usse embarrassment hui, halanki kisi aur ne dekha nahi tha. Usne correct kiya, file bheji, aur laptop band kar diya. Assessment ka answer do din baad aana tha. Aaj uske paas wait karne ke alawa kuch nahi tha.

Usne Naina ko message kiya: possible hai. Scope narrow kiya. Thanks.

Reply mein thumbs-up aur Vihaan ke homework ki photo aayi. Friend ko ek word ki spelling chahiye thi. Meera ne voice note mein batayi. Phir movie ka remaining part dekha. Ending achhi nahi lagi. Usne Naina ko yeh bhi bataya. Unki conversation phir kaam se bahar nikal gayi.

Wednesday ko test ka answer aaya. Unhone doosre editor ko choose kiya tha. Mail mein Meera ke work ki specific criticism nahi thi. Bas experience better aligned likha tha. Usne reply draft kiya: could you share brief feedback? Phir send kiya. Answer aayega ya nahi, usse nahi pata tha.

Uske baad usne browser ki tab close kar di. Rate defend karke project milne ki jo chhoti kahaani usne mann mein bana li thi, woh nahi hui. Usse bura laga. Woh sofa par baithi aur half an hour kuch nahi kiya. Phone par Naina ka contact khola, phir band kiya. Har disappointment ko immediately report karne ka mann nahi tha.

Shaam ko ek school workbook ka small assignment aaya. Rate lower tha, scope fixed. Twenty pages, references nahi, no new writing. Meera ne sample dekha. Agar efficiently kare toh acceptable tha. Usne haan kaha, delivery do din baad. Publisher ne next morning kaha kal chahiye. Meera ne original deadline ka screenshot bheja. Unhone poocha partial batch possible? Usne ten pages next evening offer kiye. Woh agree hue.

Yeh conversation teen short mails mein hui. Meera ne uske baad calendar mein ten pages aur next ten alag daale. Woh apne next client ko kabhi friction nahi hoga ka promise nahi kar sakti thi. Aaj woh time badhne se pehle keh paayi thi. Usne dinner break bhi schedule kiya, phir apne banaye break se fifteen minutes late uthi.

Naina ne raat ko sample ka poocha toh usne straight bataya, nahi mila. Friend ne kaha that sucks. Meera ne kaha haan. Dono ne usse lesson nahi banaya. Phir Meera ne workbook ka bataya. Naina ne kaha decent bridge hai. Meera ne apni copy ke margin par wrong answer circle kiya aur kaha, bridge mein potholes hain.

Woh hase. Call band hone ke baad usne second referral ka mail bheja. Agle week ek aur short project mila, travel articles ka. Dono jobs milkar lost retainer se kam the. Budget ki sheet phir kholi. Usne numbers update kiye, old amount ko pretend karke nahi chhoda. March ke liye ab bhi ek blank line thi. Usne uske saamne question mark lagaya aur file save ki.

***

Tuesday ki class mein Aarav ka project file open nahi hua.

Usne upload galat format mein kiya tha. Instructor ne screen share par error dikhaya. Group ke teen log wait kar rahe the. Aarav ne kaha two minutes. Phir galti se old version khol diya. Uski ears garam hone lagi.

"Main baad mein bhejta hoon," usne kaha.

Young teammate Rohan ne poocha local copy hai? Aarav ne haan kaha. Rohan ne bola folder share karo, main convert kar deta hoon. Uska pehla impulse tha mana kare. Apni file convert karna bhi nahi aata, aisa lagega. Phir screen par clock dekhi. Baaki log uski reputation ke baare mein nahi, assignment ke baare mein soch rahe the.

Usne folder share kiya.

Rohan ne file convert ki. Ek sheet mein formulas break hue. Aarav ne woh fix kiye. Ten minutes gaye, lekin presentation ho gayi. Instructor ne ek assumption par sawal kiya. Aarav ko answer nahi pata tha. Usne kaha check karke bataoonga. Class aage badh gayi.

Session ke baad usne Rohan ko thanks message kiya. Reply aaya: no worries, next time PDF backup. Aarav ne likha haan. Phir apni checklist banayi. Main file. PDF backup. Shared folder. Itna simple lag raha tha ab. Usne khud ko stupid kehna shuru kiya, phir list save karke band kar di.

Phone par Kabir ka message tha. Weekend par daughter ka science display tha, school parents ke friends allow kar raha tha. Aayega? Aarav ne pehle maybe type kiya. Saturday ki class assignment baaki thi. Phir actual time poocha. Display eleven se one. Woh noon tak jaa sakta tha.

Usne haan kaha, twelve tak. Kabir ne address bheja. Aarav ne calendar entry banaayi. Is baar usne travel ka time bhi rakha.

Class ki chair se uthkar usne stiff back stretch ki. Kitchen mein sink full tha. Dinner ka dabba khula tha. Use apni life kisi completed project jaisi nahi lag rahi thi. Usne dal garam ki, plates mein se ek saaf ki, aur baaki ke liye paani chadha diya. Assignment ka unknown answer notebook mein tha. Woh subah dekh sakta tha.
