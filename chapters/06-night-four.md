# Chapter 6 — Night Four: The Love We Learned as Children

Sunday ko Aarav ke Papa ne newspaper ka photo bheja.

Circle kiya hua advertisement tha: small moving services, local and intercity. Neeche ek phone number. Aarav ne thanks likha aur bataya mover booked hai. Papa ne thumbs-up bheja. Phir ek aur message: insurance pooch lena.

Aarav balcony ka drain saaf kar raha tha. Leaves jam gayi thi; Saturday raat ki baarish se paani railing ke paas tak aaya tha. Usne rubber glove pehna, phir glove mein chhed se paani andar aa gaya. Usne irritation mein glove utaar diya.

Meera ne door se poocha, "Tools kahan rakhe?"

"Neeche drawer."

"Tumne kal nikale the."

"Guest room. Bed par."

"Haan."

Woh chali gayi. Aarav ne drain cover lift kiya. Kuch black sludge nikli, ek old cable tie aur cigarette ka filter. Woh smoke nahi karta tha. Upar ke kisi flat se aaya hoga. Usse ek specific neighbour par doubt hua, phir yaad aaya sab society meetings mein ek doosre par aise hi doubt karte the.

Phone ring hua. Papa.

Usne wet hand T-shirt par pochha aur call uthaya. "Haan, Papa. Mover booked hai. Bataya toh."

"Uske liye nahi."

"Achha."

"Woh jo kal photo bheja tha prescription ka, dikh raha hai?"

"Kaunsa prescription?"

"Teri Ma ko forward karna tha. Galti se tere ko gaya hoga. Delete kar diya."

Aarav ne screen dekhi. Deleted message subah tha.

"Doctor ke paas gaye the?"

"Bas knee."

"Kab?"

"Kal."

"Toh bataya kyun nahi?"

"Batane jaisa kuch nahi. Exercise di hai."

Background mein Ma ki awaaz aayi: swelling bhi toh hai. Papa ne phone se hat kar kuch kaha jo clear nahi hua.

Aarav ne drain ka cover side mein rakha. "Prescription bhejo."

"Kyun? Doctor toh dekh liya."

"Bas bhejo."

Uski awaaz order jaisi ho gayi. Meera kitchen ki taraf jaate hue ruki. Aarav ne usse dekha, phir mud gaya.

Papa ne kaha theek hai, bhejta hoon. Phir poocha Thursday ko car use karoge ya truck ke saath jaana hai. Aarav ne kaha decide nahi. Unhone kaha car pehle park kar lena, truck ke peeche mat phasna. Aarav ko us practical detail se ek saath affection aur frustration hui.

"Papa, knee ka photo bhejna. Car manage ho jayegi."

Udhar thoda silence hua. Phir Papa ne kaha, "Theek hai."

Call band hua. Meera ab bhi kitchen doorway mein thi.

"Swelling?" usne poocha.

"Haan. Bolte nahi. Phir message galat chala jaata hai toh pata chalta hai."

"Doctor ne kya kaha?"

"Prescription aa raha hai."

Usne phone counter par rakha. Drain ka paani nikal gaya tha. Balcony mein leaves ki smell thi. Usne bucket se thoda clean water baha diya aur brush se floor saaf kiya. Chhota kaam tha, lekin poori subah usme lag gayi.

Meera chai laayi toh usne wet chair ki taraf dekha.

"Andar," usne kaha.

Woh dono dining table par baithe. Meera ke haath mein screwdriver tha. Shelf ka bracket nikaalna tha. Aarav ne kaha woh kar dega. Usne kaha ek screw already nikal gaya, second tight hai. Aarav ne screwdriver liya, dekha wrong head tha.

"Isse ghis jayega," usne kaha.

"Doosra mila nahi."

Usne toolbox khola, sahi screwdriver nikala. Meera ne use le liya.

Phone par prescription aayi. Aarav ne zoom kiya. Handwriting samajh nahi aayi. Ma ko call kiya. Papa ne uthaya. Usne kaha Ma se baat karni hai. Papa ne bulaya, background mein unki awaaz thodi chhoti lag rahi thi.

Ma ne swelling batayi, medication ka naam padha, appointment ka follow-up bataya. Aarav ne reminders set karne shuru kiye. Woh agle Sunday Nashik aane ki baat karne laga.

"Abhi tumhara bhi toh..." Ma ruki.

"Main aa sakta hoon."

"Dekho. Jaldi nahi. Doctor ne rest kaha hai."

Usne haan kaha. Meera screw ke saath struggle kar rahi thi. Aarav ne use haath se signal diya ki de do. Usne head shake kiya. Thoda pressure badla, screw nikal gaya. Metal bracket table par rakha toh chhoti loud chhan hui.

Ma ne poocha Meera hai kya.

Woh freeze hua. "Haan."

"Baat karao."

Aarav ne phone uski taraf badhaya. Uske face par surprise aaya, phir usne screwdriver rakh diya aur phone liya.

"Hello, Ma."

Aarav utha. Usse achanak samajh nahi aa raha tha ki woh kitni door jaaye. Privacy dena aur awkwardly room chhodna ek hi movement mein ho raha tha. Woh kitchen mein cup rinse karne laga.

Meera ne knee ke baare mein poocha. Phir apne move ke baare mein answers diye. Uski awaaz calm thi. Aarav words nahi sun paaya, bas pauses. Ek baar usne kaha, "Nahi, aisa nahi hai."

Uske haath rinse karte hue ruk gaye.

Thodi der baad Meera ne phone counter par rakha. "Baat kar li."

"Kya boli?"

"Flat ka poocha. Khana kaise karoongi."

"Aur?"

"Aur poocha tum time par kha lete ho na."

Aarav ne cup rack mein rakh diya. "Sorry."

"Unhone bura nahi kaha."

"Phir bhi."

Meera ne screwdriver uthaya. "Unhe aadat hai. Mujhe bhi."

Woh shelf ke paas chali gayi. Aarav ne phone apni pocket mein rakha. Ma ko message bhejna tha, phir usne socha pehle dekh le Meera upset toh nahi. Phir khud par irritate hua ki har cheez ka sequence itna difficult kyun hai.

***

Meera ko Sunanda ki awaaz mein dukh sunai diya tha, lekin usne use apni responsibility nahi banana chaha.

Yeh pehli baar nahi tha. Shaadi ke baad jab bhi Aarav unwell hota, Ma usse message karti: usse kaadha de dena. Aarav direct answer nahi deta toh use poochti: uska meeting ho gaya? Meera ne kabhi formally object nahi kiya. Kabhi-kabhi use achha bhi lagta tha ki family ka part hai, unhe us par trust hai.

Aaj woh role bina announcement ke uske haath se nikal raha tha. Aur use relieved bhi hona tha, guilty bhi.

Usne shelf ke side panel se screw nikala. Paint ka chhota tukda saath aaya. Neeche wall ka purana colour tha. Ek square inch pale green. Current cream ke andar chhupa hua.

Shaila ka phone aaya toh usne speaker nahi kiya.

"New flat mein mandir ki jagah hai?" Ma ne poocha.

"Chhoti shelf hai."

"North-east dekh lena."

Meera ne wall ka hole dekha. "Ma, abhi plug points ka dekh rahi hoon. Direction baad mein."

Ma ne kaha woh bas keh rahi hain. Meera ne haan kaha. Phir Ma ne rent agreement ki copy maangi. Safety ke liye. Usne bhejne ka promise kiya.

"Aarav ke parents ko bataya?"

"Haan."

"Kya kaha unhone?"

"Aap jaisa."

"Main toh bas pooch rahi hoon."

"Pata hai."

Ma ne poocha Wednesday ko video call ho sakti hai? Meera ne kaha dekhegi. Phir Ma ne woh sentence bola jiska use teen din se darr tha: "Beta, thoda adjust toh har aurat—"

"Ma."

Uski awaaz expected se tez nikli. Aarav ne living room se dekha. Meera balcony mein chali gayi.

"Maine bas—"

"Please aaj nahi."

"Tumhe har baat buri lagne lagi."

"Aapko har baat mein lagta hai main aur thoda kar leti toh theek ho jaata."

"Maine aisa nahi kaha."

"Par matlab wahi hai."

Woh railing ke paas khadi thi. Floor abhi damp tha. Usne ek pair dry tile par rakha. Ma chup thi.

"Papa hote toh kya kehte?" Meera ke muh se nikal gaya.

Usne turant aankhein band ki. Yeh fair nahi tha. Papa ko gaye saal ho chuke the. Woh conversation mein aakar uski side nahi le sakte the.

Ma ne dheere kaha, "Mujhe nahi pata."

Meera ne railing pakdi. "Sorry. Yeh nahi bolna tha."

Udhar ek long breath aayi. "Main tumhare khilaaf nahi hoon."

"Pata hai."

"Nahi pata tumhe. Akeli rahogi toh raat ko bukhaar aaye toh kaun—"

"Main Naina ko call kar sakti hoon. Doctor ko. Aapko."

"Phone aur paas hona alag hai."

Meera ko yeh baat hurt kar gayi kyunki usme kuch sach tha. Woh har sach ka practical answer nahi bana paayi.

"Haan," usne kaha. "Alag hai."

Ma ne kuch der baad poocha ki curtain rods hain ya naye lagwaane hain. Meera ne current tenant ki photos kholi. Rods thi. Curtains nahi. Ma ne kaha spare bhej sakti hain. Meera ne mana nahi kiya.

Call khatam hone se pehle usne kaha, "Agreement bhej rahi hoon. Par please Aarav ko abhi call karke kuch samjhana mat."

Ma ne kaha, "Main use kyun call karoongi?"

Meera ne jawab nahi diya. Phir kaha, "Bas keh diya."

"Theek hai. Nahi karoongi."

Usne phone neeche kiya. Thodi der balcony mein rahi. Street par vegetable cart aa rahi thi. Bhindi, tamatar, methi. Usne socha neeche jaakar bhindi le aaye. Phir yaad aaya fridge mein enough vegetables thi. Ordinary thought hone se use relief mila.

***

Dinner ke liye Aarav rotis garam kar raha tha. Meera ne dal mein lemon ki jagah nimbu alag rakhne ko kaha. Usne wedge bowl mein rakhi. Table ke bracket loose hone se plate zara hilli. Dono ne dekha. Kisi ne abhi fix karne ki baat nahi ki.

"Ma ne kuch kaha?" Aarav ne poocha.

Meera ne apni plate mein dal li. "Adjust."

Woh ruk gaya.

"Unhe darr lag raha hai," usne add kiya. "Mujhe gussa aa raha hai. Dono chal raha hai."

Aarav ne roti uski taraf ki. "Meri Ma bhi darr rahi hongi."

"Haan."

Usne poocha, "Tumhe bura laga unhone mere khane ka poocha?"

Meera thodi der chup rahi. "Thoda. Par main eight years se answer karti aayi hoon. Unhe suddenly kaise..."

Woh sentence chhod kar paani peene lagi.

Aarav ne bachpan ka dining table socha. Laminate ka top tha, corner par chip. Papa office se aate toh Sunanda pehle poochti chai. Phir bag leti. Aarav school mein kya hua, woh Ma ko afternoon mein bata chuka hota. Papa tak wahi version jaata jo dinner ke beech Ma ko yaad aata.

Ek baar Aarav ne district quiz jeeta tha. Certificate lekar woh gate par khada tha. Papa aaye, shoes khole, certificate dekha. Poocha next round ke liye fees hai? Aarav ne kaha nahi. Papa ne haan kaha, tap par muh dhoya aur dining table par baith gaye.

Us din usse laga tha jeetna utna special nahi tha. Do din baad stationery shop wale uncle ne free pen diya. Bole, tumhare Papa bata rahe the first prize.

Ghar aakar Aarav ne Papa se kuch nahi poocha. Usne pen school bag mein rakha. Bahut carefully.

Meera ne poocha, "Kya hua?"

Woh current table par wapas aaya. "Quiz ka certificate yaad aa gaya. School wala."

"Tumhare cupboard mein tha. Blue folder."

"Haan. Papa ne mere saamne kuch nahi kaha. Pados mein sabko bata diya."

Meera ne smile ki. "Proud the."

"Haan. Par tab sunna tha."

Uske baad kuch der sirf plates ki awaaz thi. Aarav ko laga yeh baat kahne se woh apne Papa ko unfairly present kar raha hai. Unhone bike bechi thi uski college fees ke liye. Raat bhar railway station par wait kiya tha jab admission interview se laut raha tha. Ek certificate ka response in sabko cancel nahi karta tha.

"Bure father nahi the," usne kaha.

"Maine nahi kaha."

"Main bas..."

"Pata hai."

Meera ne roti ka last bite liya. Phir kaha, "Mere ghar mein bolte bahut the. Par har cheez bol sakte the, aisa bhi nahi."

Aarav ne uski taraf dekha.

"Ma ko bolti thi aaj friend ke yahan jaana hai, toh pehle unhe feel hota tha unke saath time nahi chahiye. Phir main reason bada banati thi. Group project. Important. Bas hang out karna valid nahi tha."

"Ab bhi karti ho," uske muh se nikla.

Meera seedhi baith gayi.

"Reason bada," usne clarify kiya. "Agar akela rehna ho toh headache bolti ho."

Usne socha woh deny karegi. Meera ne table ki edge dekhi. "Haan. Tum bhi toh agar main boloon aaj baat nahi karni, poochte ho kyun."

"Normal sawal hai."

"Haan. Par mere liye uska answer ready hona chahiye."

Aarav ko yeh exhausting laga. Usne tum complicated ho kehne ka mann roka. Usse bhi har sawal kabhi criticism lagta tha. Normal sawal ka hona uske experience ko normal nahi bana deta.

"Main bina bataye chup ho jaata hoon," usne kaha.

"Mujhe pata chalta hai."

Uski awaaz dry thi. Aarav ne dekha, woh mazaak kar rahi thi. Thoda.

"Papa bhi ho jaate the," usne kaha. "Ghar mein sab dheere chalte the phir."

"Mere Papa loud ho jaate the," Meera ne kaha. "Phir next morning poochte dosa khaogi, jaise..."

Usne sentence adhura chhod diya. Aarav ne usse complete nahi kiya. Woh Shaila ke ghar gaya tha. Unke breakfasts cheerful hote the. Usse pata nahi tha unke peeche kabhi koi unfinished evening bhi hoti thi.

Meera ne dal ka bacha hua bowl fridge mein rakha. Aarav ne plates li. Woh dono kitchen mein ek doosre ke aas-paas move karte rahe. Kabhi shoulder touch ho jaata toh apne aap sorry nikalta. Ek waqt tha isi kitchen mein space kam hone par woh uski waist par haath rakh kar side se nikal jaata. Ab distance maintain karne mein bartan ki jagah badalni padti thi.

Usne tap khola. Meera ne kaha, "Paani waste ho raha."

Woh turant chidh gaya. "Abhi toh khola."

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. Ek pal ko dono ruk gaye. Phir Aarav ne tap kam kiya. Water pressure waise bhi bahut tez tha.

"Haan. Sorry," usne kaha.

Meera ne answer mein lecture nahi diya. Cloth uthaya aur table pochhne chali gayi.

Aarav ko sharm aayi. Abhi woh itni calmly childhood discuss kar raha tha, aur ek tap par irritate ho gaya. Uske andar kisi fundamental change ki awaaz nahi thi. Bas dish soap ki smell aur wet sleeves thi.

Usne plates finish ki. Phone nikala. Ma ka message tha ki Papa exercises nahi kar rahe. Usne Papa ko call karne ke bajaye Ma ko reply likha: aap unse karwa lo.

Woh sentence dekhta raha.

Delete kiya. Papa ko call kiya.

"Exercises kar rahe ho?"

"Abhi khaya."

"Kal morning reminder laga lo."

"Mujhe yaad hai."

"Theek. Ma ko bolne ki zaroorat na pade."

Phone par Papa ki saans sunai di. Aarav ko laga woh zyada bol gaya. Phir Papa ne poocha, "Tera khana hua?"

"Haan."

"Achha."

Aarav ne unhe bataya ki Thursday full leave li hai. Mover ka insurance check kar liya. Knee ka follow-up Friday tha, uska reminder calendar mein daala. Papa ne haan kaha. Conversation familiar cheezon par laut aayi.

Call rakhne se pehle Aarav ne kaha, "Subah newspaper wala number bheja, thanks."

"Kaam nahi aaya."

"Phir bhi. Dekha aapne."

Papa ne seedha jawab nahi diya. Poocha woh number delete kar doon? Aarav ne kaha rehne do, kisi aur ke kaam aa jayega. Papa ne haan kaha.

Usne phone rakh diya. Living room mein Meera tools count kar rahi thi. Chaar screws, do brackets. Usne ek chhoti envelope mein daale aur shelf ke panel par tape kiye.

"Monday library ko call?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Haan. Reminder hai."

Usne aur nahi kaha. Meera ne marker cap ki. Phir poocha, "Tumhare Papa theek?"

"Haan. Exercises nahi kar rahe."

Uske muh par woh familiar irritation aa gayi hogi. Meera ne kaha, "Unse bolna video call par karo. Do din. Phir unhe tumse bachne ke liye khud karna padega."

Aarav hansa. Usne kaha, "Yeh work kar sakta hai."

"Meri Ma par nahi karta. Unhe company mil jaati hai."

Woh dono thodi der family ke chhote quirks par baat karte rahe. Sunanda ka har bag mein safety pin rakhna. Shaila ka voice message mein pehle kehna do minute hain, phir eight-minute recording. Prakash ka phone case mein train ticket rakhna, jab ticket phone mein hi ho.

Meera ne ek baar kaha, "Main bhi voice note ke start mein yahi bolti hoon."

Aarav ne haan mein sir hilaya. Use pehle bhi pata tha. Usne kabhi bataya nahi tha.

***

Raat ko phone par Shaila ka message aaya. Meera ne dining table par use khola. Curtains ki photo thi. Do sets: green aur brown. Neeche poocha tha kaunsa.

Meera ne green choose kiya. Phir ek aur message likha: address kal bhejoongi. Aur Wednesday video call 7 baje kar sakte hain. Fifteen minutes.

Usne phone rakh diya. Aarav ne usse poocha kya smile kar rahi ho.

"Curtains," usne kaha.

"Achhe hain?"

"Brown bahut bure hain."

"Toh green liye?"

"Haan."

Usne phir apne bracket wali envelope dekhi. Tape ka end uth raha tha. Use dobara press kiya. Is baar achhi tarah chipak gaya.
