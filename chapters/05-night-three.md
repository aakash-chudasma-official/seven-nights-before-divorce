# Chapter 5 — Night Three: The People We Used to Be

Saturday subah Meera ne apna purana office ID dustbin mein daala, phir nikaal liya.

Photo achhi nahi thi. Card ka plastic yellow ho gaya tha. Neeche designation ka ek letter ghis gaya tha. Assistant Editor ab Assistant Edito dikhta tha, jaise uske career ka last part bhi kahin utar gaya ho.

Usne khud ko us comparison se roka. Bas purana card tha. Har cheez par sentence banane ki zaroorat nahi.

Card ko documents wale folder mein rakha. Dustbin mein paper wrappers, old elastic bands aur teen bookmarks the jo ads ke liye print hue the. Useful books ek side. Keep wale books doosri side. Jo books sirf isliye rakh rahi thi kyunki unhe kabhi padhegi, unka pile sabse bada tha.

Aarav market gaya tha. Jaane se pehle usne Thursday ki leave batayi. Meera ne haan kaha, phir thanks. Woh fridge ke paas khada tha, jaise second word sunne se pehle move nahi kar paa raha ho. Usne khud ko remind kiya ki har pause ko interpret karna zaroori nahi.

Usne Naina ki sample file kholi. Rate kam tha. Itna kam nahi ki insult ho, itna zyada bhi nahi ki uske hours cover ho. Woh pehle bol deti, theek hai, kar loongi. Phir deadline ke time irritate hoti. Is baar usne scope ke teen questions bheje aur kaha final quote answers ke baad degi.

Send karte hi anxiety hui. Kaam kisi aur ko de diya toh? Usne phone face down rakha. Teen seconds baad check kiya. Blue tick nahi tha.

Shelf ke lower corner mein uska black notebook mila.

Cover par chai ka circular daag tha. Usne khola toh pehle page par shop names the. After Hours. Open Page. Ek naam ko teen lines se kaata tha, kyunki Naina ne kaha stationery shop lag raha hai. Neeche pencil mein shelves ka sketch tha. Tall window, do chairs, return box. Last pages mein numbers.

Rent estimate. Deposit ka blank. Stock purchase ke liye amount likha tha, lekin stock sell na ho toh next month kya, woh blank. Aarav ne red pen se question mark lagaya tha. Use yaad tha woh question mark kitna rude laga tha.

Ab bhi rude lag raha tha.

Door khula. Aarav do grocery bags lekar aaya. Ek mein paper towels the, doosre mein potatoes aur refill pouch. Meera ko usse kehna pada ki potatoes already hain. Woh turant fridge ki taraf gaya, phir ruka.

"List mein the," usne kaha.

"Purani list hogi."

"Haan."

Usne extra potatoes counter par rakh diye aur khaali bag fold karne laga.

"Coffee?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Abhi nahi."

Woh books ke paas baith gaya. "Yeh job wali books bhi le jaogi?"

"Job wali?"

"Publishing. Yeh manuals."

"Kaam abhi bhi wahi hai."

Usne head shake kiya. "Haan. Mera matlab office wali."

Meera ne notebook band ki. Aarav ne dekh liya tha.

"After Hours," usne cover ki taraf ishara kiya. "Abhi bhi naam pasand hai?"

"Pata nahi."

"Mujhe achha laga tha."

"Tumne bola tha bar lagega."

"Woh bhi achha ho sakta hai."

Usne smile ki. Meera ko bhi hansi aayi. Phir usne notebook seedha box mein rakh diya.

"Plan kyun rakh rahi ho phir?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Kyunki mera hai."

Uske face par smile kam hui. "Maine throw karne ko nahi kaha."

Meera ne palm notebook par rakhi. "Sorry."

Usne floor par baithne ki position badli. Knee mein crack hua. "Main bhi old ho raha hoon."

"Tumhara knee twenty-seven mein bhi aise hi karta tha."

"Toh consistent hoon."

Meera ne notebook wapas nikaali. "Numbers bahut kharab the."

Aarav use dekhne laga. "Itne bhi nahi."

"Deposit nahi tha."

"Haan."

"Inventory twice count ki thi."

"Woh tha."

Uski awaaz mein familiarity thi. Usne sach mein file dekhi thi. Meera ko kabhi yeh yaad karne ka mann nahi karta tha, kyunki uske baad jo hua tha woh yaad karna aasaan nahi tha.

***

October 2024 mein notebook nayi thi.

Meera ne table se plates hata kar laptop khola tha. Dinner ke baad woh Aarav ko spreadsheet dikhana chahti thi. Usne pehle message nahi kiya tha ki proper discussion chahiye. Use laga tha ghar par hai toh baat ho jayegi.

Aarav warehouse call se nikla tha. Shirt badal kar aaya, spoon se ice cream nikaali aur poocha, "Kya dekhna hai?"

Usne shuru mein name bataya. Phir concept. Log office ke baad aa sakte the. Full shop se pehle chhota reading space. Books for sale, memberships maybe, monthly events. Uske ex-colleague ne bola tha ek commercial unit khaali hai. Window achhi thi.

Aarav ne poocha footfall kitna hai.

Meera ne kaha residential area hai, potential hai.

Usne poocha monthly break-even.

Meera ne cell dikhaya.

Usne deposit poocha.

Meera ne kaha estimate mein include karegi.

"Aur unsold stock?" usne poocha.

Usne kaha pehle consignment try karegi. Aarav ne laptop apni taraf kiya, ek formula change kiya, phir kaha, "Meera, yeh abhi business plan nahi hai."

Woh sentence sach tha. Us shaam Meera ne sirf uski awaaz suni thi.

"Maine kab kaha final hai?"

"Tum unit dekhne gayi thi. Token dogi toh—"

"Main dekhne gayi thi. Shaadi nahi ki usse."

Aarav ko sarcasm pasand nahi aaya. Usne laptop ghuma diya. "Main bas numbers bol raha hoon."

"Tum bas numbers hi bolte ho."

Usne ice cream ki bowl side ki. "Paise kahin se toh aayenge."

"Maine tumse maange?"

"Joint savings use karogi toh haan."

Woh joint savings thi. Meera ko pata tha. Pichhle do saal uske contributions uneven the. Aarav ne kabhi straight usse kam contribute karne ka taana nahi diya tha. Lekin us line mein use woh bhi sunai diya jo nahi bola gaya tha.

"Toh mere paise hain hi nahi?"

"Maine kab kaha?"

Unhone us raat formula ya deposit par dobara baat nahi ki. Meera ne laptop band kar diya. Aarav ne kaha, "Aise kaise discuss karoon?"

Usne jawab diya, "Mat karo."

Agli subah Aarav ne ek article bheja: small businesses that survive the first year. Meera ne open nahi kiya.

Usse teen din baad Naina ne local library ke coordinator ka number diya. Woh weekend reading club try kar rahe the. Space free, revenue share, chhota start. Meera ne number save kiya, profile photo dekhi, phir phone rakh diya. Usne socha tha yeh real shop nahi hai. Use apni idea ka cheap version nahi chahiye tha.

Woh number ab bhi phone mein tha.

***

Meera ne notebook ka ek page fold kiya. "Library wala pilot kar sakti thi," usne kaha.

Aarav ne poocha, "Kaunsa pilot?"

"Naina ne contact diya tha. Weekends ke liye."

"Tumne bataya nahi."

"Nahi."

Usne jawab ke baad safai nahi di. Woh box ke cardboard edge par nail chalaati rahi.

"Kyun nahi kiya?" Aarav ne poocha.

Meera ko ek achha answer chahiye tha. Money, time, family. Teeno kuch had tak sach the. Lekin us particular week phone na karne ki wajah kuch aur thi.

"Mujhe chhota laga," usne kaha. "Apne plan se."

Woh chup raha.

"Stupid hai."

"Nahi."

"Thoda toh hai."

"Ho sakta hai."

Usne Aarav ko dekha. Woh smile nahi kar raha tha. Isliye Meera hansi. "Tumhe thoda reassurance dena aata nahi."

"Abhi training chal rahi hai."

Uske joke ke baad dono ki smile jaldi chali gayi. Meera ne socha woh aaj ke har sentence ko repair ki rehearsal bana denge toh bahut exhausting hoga.

"Main job chhodte waqt bhi bahut sure thi," usne kaha.

"Humne decide kiya tha."

"Haan. Par main bhi chahti thi. Yeh main baad mein bhool gayi."

Publisher ne reduced role offer kiya tha. Lower salary, more contract work. Meera hurt thi. Aath logon ke office mein usne years diye the; suddenly unka statement tha industry badal rahi hai. Aarav ne kaha tha kuch time break le lo. Usne us permission ko support maana. Woh support tha bhi. Lekin usne usse yeh nahi kaha tha ki woh agle saal tak dobara job dhoondhne ki energy kho degi. Us waqt use khud nahi pata tha.

"Main sochta tha tum relax kar rahi ho," Aarav ne kaha.

"Ghar se author ka phone aa raha ho, Ma ka physio schedule ho, aur payment ke liye reminder bhej rahi hoon, toh relax nahi."

"Haan. Ab samajh raha hoon."

"Tumhare saamne payment mail likhti thi."

Usne nazar neeche kar li. Meera ko apne sentence ka familiar edge sunai diya. Usne usse wapas nahi liya. Woh sach tha. Lekin ab usse ek aur sach bhi bolna tha.

"Maine applications bhi kam bheji," usne kaha. "Pehle salary kam thi. Phir commute bura. Phir role junior. Mujhe sab wapas start karna insult lag raha tha."

Aarav ne notebook ki blank jagah dekhi. "Junior hona bura lagta hai."

"Haan."

"Maine Bengaluru wala programme bhi isliye chhoda tha. Thoda."

Meera ki ungli ruk gayi.

"Mujhe bola tha Papa ki tabiyat aur fees."

"Woh bhi tha."

"Company part sponsor kar rahi thi."

"Half."

"Haan."

Usne seedha uski taraf dekha. "Wahan twenty-eight ke log mujhse better the. Assessment mein mera score average tha. Main office mein woh person tha jisse sab poochte the. Udhar basic questions bhi..."

Usne baat chhod di. Meera ne first time us programme ka brochure dekha tha toh use laga tha Aarav excited hai. New team, new role. Phir usne usse kaha tha postpone karte hain. Woh agree ho gayi thi kyunki us week Ma ke appointments the. Usne aage poocha nahi tha.

"Tum mujhe bol sakte the," usne kaha.

Aarav hansa nahi. "Main khud ko thodi bol raha tha."

Doorbell hui. Dono ne ek saath door dekha. Mrs D'Souza thi, building notice lekar. Service lift ka issue printed letter par bhi tha. Unhone Meera ko kaha Thursday ko baarish ho toh books cover karna. Meera ne thanks kaha. Unhone nahi poocha kahaan jaa rahi ho. Shayad notice mein move-out naam tha. Shayad unhe farq nahi padta tha. Meera ko unka yeh na poochna bahut achha laga.

Wapas floor par aayi toh Aarav grocery bags fold kar raha tha.

"Tum aaj book boxes mat seal karo," usne kaha. "Kal newspaper kam pade toh bubble wrap le aaunga."

Meera ne haan kaha. Phir use yaad aaya abhi woh uske programme par baat kar rahe the. Kya use wapas laana chahiye? Usne poocha, "Dobara apply kar sakte ho?"

"Ab role alag hai. Dekhna padega."

"Dekho."

"Haan."

Woh haan kitni baar use suna chuki thi. Meera apne phone par library contact kholne lagi. Number ab bhi saved tha: Reading pilot — Devika.

Usne message draft kiya, hello, you probably don't remember me. Delete kiya. Koi unhe remember karna required nahi tha.

Likha: Hi Devika, main Meera Sane. Naina ne aapka contact 2024 mein diya tha. Weekend reading sessions ke baare mein baat karni thi. Kya abhi bhi kisi tarah ka programme chal raha hai?

Send kiya. Phone ko upside down rakha, phir kitchen mein coffee banane gayi.

Aarav ne poocha, "Mere liye bhi?"

"Haan."

Usne do cups nikaale. Ek chhota, ek bada. Usse ab coffee ki proportion yaad thi. Phir bhi usne poocha kitni strong. Aarav ne kaha normal. Meera ne kaha normal ka matlab mere liye kuch aur hai. Usne fingers se amount dikhaya. Itna.

Coffee banne tak unhone extra potatoes ka decide kiya. Half Mrs D'Souza ko offer karenge. Woh mana karein toh weekend mein aloo bana lenge. Meera ne bade potatoes alag kiye; unke beech do chhote itne geele the ki pehle unhe use karna padega.

Phone par reply aaya.

Devika ab library mein nahi thi. Number current coordinator ka bhej diya. Neeche good luck likha tha.

Meera ne thanks bheja. New contact save kiya. Phir screen band kar di. Abhi call karne ka time nahi tha; library three baje close hoti thi. Monday reminder laga diya.

"Kya hua?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Library mein woh person change ho gayi. New number mila."

"Oh."

"Monday poochungi."

"Achha."

Usne coffee ka cup uthaya. Aarav ne baat ko successful business launch nahi bana diya. Na kaha, dekha, simple tha. Iske liye Meera usse thanks nahi bolne wali thi. Phir bhi usse relief hua.

Shaam tak books ke do boxes ready ho gaye. Ek chhote box par Meera ne WORK likha. Uske neeche marker ruk gaya. Woh EDITING likhna chahti thi, phir laga obvious hai. Phir likh diya. WORK / EDITING.

Aarav ne kaha, "Isme manuals hain toh heavy neeche rakhenge."

"Isme laptop nahi daaloongi. Woh bag mein."

"Haan."

Usne box utha kar wall ke paas rakha. Meera ko work wala label dekhkar unexpectedly achha laga. Freelance invoices, payment follow-ups, author queries. Yeh temporary filler nahi tha jab tak uski real life wapas aaye. Yeh bhi kaam tha. Thoda kam paise wala, thoda solitary, kabhi frustrating. Lekin usne kiya tha.

Raat ko unhone khichdi banayi. Aarav ne extra ginger daal diya. Meera ne thoda dahi liya. Food par discussion aaya toh dono thoda careful ho gaye. Usne almost kaha aaj rice issue nahi hai, phir nahi kaha. Har kal ki baat ko aaj ka joke banana zaroori nahi.

Dinner ke baad Aarav cupboard ke upar se guitar utarne laga.

Meera ne doorway se poocha, "Yeh abhi?"

"Bas bag dekh raha tha. Damp toh nahi."

Guitar case ka zipper atka. Usne zyada force lagaya toh Meera ne kaha candle rub kar sakte ho. Woh kitchen gaya, birthday candles ka packet laaya. Case khula. Ek string loose thi, ek bilkul snapped. Wood par halki dhool thi.

"Tumne kab bajaya last?" Meera ne poocha.

"Pata nahi."

"Housewarming?"

"Uske baad bhi. Maybe."

Usne string ko side kiya. Meera ne haath badhaya, phir roka. Usse guitar handle karna aata nahi tha. Aarav ne use apni lap mein rakha. Ek chord press ki. Awaaz galat thi.

"Tuning gayi," usne kaha.

"Achha hai. Mujhe laga song tha."

Woh hansa. Aaj uska hasna kal raat se thoda alag tha. Meera ne ise diagnose nahi kiya.

"Repair kara lo," usne kaha.

"Haan."

Woh doorway mein aur thodi der rahi. Usse laga kehna chahiye abhi appointment kar lo, warna phir rakh doge. Usne nahi kaha. Room mein guitar, uske box, uski packing, uska next week, sab alag-alag kaam the. Har kaam ka reminder use nahi banna tha.

Apne bedroom mein usne notebook kholi. Shop names ke peeche ek blank page tha. Us par new coordinator ka number likha. Budget nahi badla. Window ab bhi sketch thi. Monday ka reminder phone par bhi tha.

Usne office ID notebook mein bookmark ki tarah rakh diya.

Phir nikala. Alag folder mein rakha. Card ki sharp edge se page kharab ho sakta tha.
