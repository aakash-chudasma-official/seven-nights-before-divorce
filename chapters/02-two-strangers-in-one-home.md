# Chapter 2 — Two Strangers in One Home

Packing tape ki awaaz poore flat mein jaati thi.

Meera ne strip box ke neeche chipkayi aur scissors ke liye haath badhaya. Scissors table par reh gayi thi. Usne tape ko daanton se kaatne ki koshish ki. Plastic zara sa stretch hua aur uske upper lip par chipak gaya.

"Great," usne khud se kaha.

Kitchen ke paas Aarav ne sar uthaya.

"Kya?"

"Kuch nahi. Scissors dena."

Woh le aaya. Meera ne use lete hue aankhein neeche rakhi, kyunki abhi uske muh par tape ka chhota transparent tukda laga tha.

"Tumhare—"

"Pata hai."

Usne tukda nikala. Aarav ki ek hansi nikal gayi. Bahut chhoti. Meera ne use dekhkar kaha, "Tum karo phir."

"De do."

Box uski taraf sarakaya toh usne galat flap pehle fold ki.

"Chhoti wali andar."

"Pata hai."

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. Woh khud bhi hansa, lekin box par aankhein rakhi.

Abhi aath bhi nahi baje the. Families ko phone karne ke baad waqt ajeeb tarah se bach gaya tha. Meera ne socha tha shaam mushkil hogi. Woh hui bhi thi, lekin uske baad raat ko karne ke liye bartan, ek overdue proof aur cardboard boxes the.

Usne floor par newspaper bichhaya. Books ke liye damp floor risk nahi lena tha. Khidki ke paas baarish ki halki boondein aayi thi; usne subah pocha lagane wale ko balcony dry chhodne ko kaha tha. Uske baad khud window khol di thi. Ab newsprint bhi thoda geela tha.

"Yeh wala side hata do," usne kaha.

"Abhi toh bichhaya."

"Geela hai."

Aarav ne newspaper uthaya aur achanak usme se ek cockroach nikal aaya.

Meera peeche hati. Aarav khud bhi hata, phir dono ne dekha ki woh asli nahi tha. Dark brown leaf tha, baarish mein chipka hua.

"Dono bahut independent reh lenge," Meera ke muh se nikal gaya.

Aarav ne leaf dustpan mein daala. "Main pest control kara loonga."

Woh mazaak kar raha tha ya seriously plan bana raha tha, Meera ko samajh nahi aaya. Dono possibilities uski thi.

Usne pehla book box sofa ke paas rakha. Usme abhi sirf reference dictionaries aur kuch hardbacks jaane the. Shelf ke middle section se ek style manual nikala toh andar se bank ka purana envelope gira.

Woh envelopes bookmarks ki tarah use karti thi. Aarav receipts use karta tha. Ek baar electricity bill novel mein daal kar teen din dhoondha tha; usne finally duplicate download kiya tha aur purani copy do mahine baad mil gayi thi.

Us waqt unhone us baat par kitna hansa tha, Meera ko yaad nahi tha. Bas yeh yaad tha ki Aarav ne use irresponsible kaha tha toh usne kaha tha bill time par pay ho gaya, paper ka kya karoge. Aarav ne jawab nahi diya tha.

Har chhoti memory ke saath koi aur chhoti cheez chipki thi. Woh decide nahi kar pa rahi thi ki packing mein sabko utarna zaroori hai ya nahi.

Table par uska laptop ping hua.

Client ne corrected pages bheje the, subject mein final likha tha. Meera ne open kiya. Teen pages ka text reflow ho gaya tha; ek running head galat tha. Usne cursor note par rakha aur likha: please don't reset the accepted corrections in the next version.

Sentence delete kiya. Likha: page 48 par pichhle round ki corrections restore karni hongi.

Aarav box ko tape kar raha tha. Strip thodi tirchhi gayi, phir usne doosri strip lagayi. Meera use kehti ki double tape kaafi hai, lekin abhi woh publisher ko respectful rehne mein apni energy laga rahi thi.

"Tumhara kaam kitne baje tak?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Pata nahi."

"Approx? Main books—"

"Aadha ghanta. Maybe."

Woh sir hila kar shelves ke paas chala gaya.

Page forty-eight par ek footnote poori delete ho gayi thi. Meera ne apni copy kholi. Usne woh note restore kiya, query bheji, phir phone mein Naina ka voice message dekha. Headphones lagaye bina sunna theek nahi laga. Usne earbud dhoondha. Ek charging case mein tha. Doosra work chair ke neeche.

"Yeh tum rakhogi?" Aarav ne door se poocha.

Uske haath mein tall blue vase tha.

"Abhi nahi. Kaam kar rahi hoon."

"Bas pooch raha tha."

"Baad mein poochna."

Usne vase rakh diya. Awaaz expected se zyada tez aayi.

Meera ki nazar screen se uthi. Aarav ne kuch nahi kaha. Woh kitchen ki taraf chala gaya.

Usne headphones utar diye.

"Woh meri Ma ka diya hua hai," usne kaha.

"Okay."

"Le jaoongi."

"Okay."

Ab usse apna pehla jawab rude laga. Lekin wapas us baat par jaana bhi ek aur task tha. Usne file save ki. Ten-minute ka kaam chalis minute le gaya tha. Aarav ko screen dekhkar shayad sirf itna dikhta hoga ki woh laptop par baithi hai. Jaise use uske kaam ki sirf calls sunai deti thi, unka pressure nahi.

Naina ka voice note chaar minute ka tha. Usme separation par advice nahi thi. Publisher ne ek author ki acknowledgements se galti se author's own name hata diya tha aur Naina ab damage control kar rahi thi. Beech mein Vihaan ne paani maanga. Naina ne recording rokne ke bajaye use kaha, "Bottle tumhare bag mein hai, darling, bag tumhare saamne hai."

Meera ko bina kisi particular reason ke us voice note se relief mila.

Usne reply record kiya, "Author ko bolo self-effacing approach hai. Very literary."

Apni hansi sun kar usne recording delete nahi ki.

***

Guest room ka cupboard kholte hi Aarav ke pair ke paas ek yoga mat giri.

Uski nahi thi. Meera ki bhi shayad nahi. Usse laga kabhi Naina chhod gayi thi, phir yaad aaya woh Diwali sale mein khud laaya tha. Ek month ka fitness challenge tha. Mat ke rubber band par price sticker ab bhi chipka tha.

Room mein jagah thi, bas use banani padti. Ek single bed, printer, suitcase, extra pillows aur folding drying stand. Aarav ne stand hata kar suitcase neeche rakha. Printer ko shift kiya toh wire tight ho gayi. Usne plug nikala. Kal Meera ko print chahiye hoga toh batana padega.

Phone ring hua. Kabir.

"Sheet kal kar raha hoon," Aarav ne uthate hi kaha.

"Arre, sheet ke liye nahi. Kal nine-thirty se pehle aayega? School drop meri hai, thoda late hoonga. Agar vendor call—"

"Theek."

"Tu theek hai? Aaj three baar same attachment bheja."

"Haan."

Kabir kuch bolta, usse pehle Aarav ne kaha, "Meera shift kar rahi hai. Next week."

Udhar traffic ki awaaz aayi. Phir indicator.

"Oh," Kabir ne kaha. "Matlab... tum log?"

"Haan."

"Yaar."

Aarav ne bed par pada pillow seedha kiya.

"Baat kab hui?"

"Kaafi time se ho rahi thi."

"Tune bataya nahi."

"Ab bata raha hoon."

Uska tone kharab ho gaya. Kabir ne jawab dene mein thoda waqt liya.

"Haan. Sorry. Main... kya boloon samajh nahi aa raha."

"Kuch nahi."

"Tu aa mere ghar Sunday ko. Ya main aa jaoon?"

"Sunday packing hai."

"Okay. Kal chai. Agar mann ho."

"Office mein hi toh milenge."

"Office wali chai mein budget ki smell aati hai. Bahar."

Aarav ne mattress ke corner par haath phiraya. Chadar nahi thi.

"Dekhte hain."

Kabir ne kaha ki vendor ka call woh afternoon tak move karwa dega. Aarav ne turant mana kiya. "Kaam toh kar hi sakta hoon."

"Maine kaha tu kaam nahi kar sakta? Mere drop ka scene hai, afternoon mujhe bhi better hai."

"Oh. Haan. Karwa de."

Call ke baad woh kuch der bed par baitha raha. Kabir ko bata dene se koi weight kam nahi hua tha. Bas ab kal office mein kisi ko pata hoga ki woh kisi aur reason se chup hai.

Usne office group khola. Do messages ka reply kiya. Ek spreadsheet download ki. File open hote hi uske andar ka pressure familiar shape mein aa gaya. Wrong dispatch date. Correctable. Ten minutes.

Jab usne clock dekhi toh nine bajne wale the.

Meera door mein khadi thi. Uske haath mein folded bedsheet thi.

"Yeh cupboard se le leti," Aarav ne kaha.

"Tumne nikali nahi thi."

"Ab nikaal raha tha."

Usne sheet bed par rakhi. White background, thin grey checks. Aarav ko yaad nahi tha yeh unki thi.

"Printer unplug kiya hai," usne kaha. "Kal chahiye toh—"

"Mujhe abhi chahiye."

"Sorry. Wire chhoti thi. Main—"

"Main kar loongi."

Woh desk ki taraf gayi. Aarav khada hua. Dono ek hi waqt plug ke paas jhuke, phir dono peeche hue.

"Tum karo," usne kaha.

Meera ne plug lagaya. Printer ka green light blink hua, machinery andar kuch adjust karne lagi. Screen par low ink aaya.

"Black bhi low hai?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Haan."

"Manga lo."

Meera seedhi hui. "Manga liya."

"Achha."

Woh uski taraf dekh rahi thi, jaise sentence ke baad kuch aana tha. Aarav ko sirf ek order yaad aaya jo usne pichhle month kiya tha. "Last time main laaya tha na?"

"Haan, Aarav."

"Toh?"

"Kuch nahi. Chhodo."

Usse chid hui. "Aise mat karo. Ab kya hua?"

Meera ne door ki taraf dekha. Phir dheere kaha, "Kabhi tum khud dekhkar order kar do. Bas. Har baar mujhe bolne ki zaroorat na ho."

"Mujhe low ink dikha hi nahi."

"Ab dikha. Toh tumne mujhe bola manga lo."

Woh keh sakta tha ki usne printer kitne months se use hi nahi kiya. Yeh sahi tha. Isse baat solve nahi hoti, yeh usse andaza tha, lekin us andaze se uski irritation kam nahi hui.

"Theek hai. Next time dekh loonga."

Meera ne lips press kiye. "Ab next time ka..."

Woh ruk gayi.

Printer se pehla page nikla. Sirf upar ki teen lines clear thi. Neeche grey faded text.

Aarav ne page uthaya. Meera ne uske haath se nahi liya. Usne print cancel kiya aur kaha, "Kal bahar karwa loongi."

"Main office se—"

"Nahi. Rental documents hain. Main karwa loongi."

Is baar usne offer repeat nahi kiya.

Uske jaane ke baad Aarav ne sheet kholi. Do corners andar ki taraf folded the. Usne ek end mattress ke neeche kiya, doosri side kheechi; pehla corner bahar aa gaya. Single bed ki sheet double lag rahi thi. Usne extra fabric side mein tuck kar diya.

Shelf par ek chhota sa alarm clock tha. Battery khatam thi. Printer, clock, mat, suitcase. Is room mein unka woh samaan rehta tha jise har din zaroorat nahi padti thi. Ab uska toothbrush bhi yahan aane wala tha.

Woh bathroom gaya. Meera ne sink ke neeche se ek spare mug nikaal kar uske brush ke liye rakh diya tha. Blue mug. Rim par chip tha. Usne apna brush uthaya, phir mug ki taraf dekha.

Yeh cup woh chai ke liye use karta tha jab dono mugs dirty hote. Meera kehti thi chip muh par lag sakta hai. Woh kehta tha doosri side se peeta hoon.

Ab woh toothbrush ke liye safe tha.

***

Meera ne client ki file bheji aur laptop shutdown kiya. Living room ab bhi utna hi bhara hua tha. Ek box ready hua tha. Do ulte pade the. Books abhi shelf mein thi.

Usne inventory notebook kholi.

Ek column: le jaana. Doosra: rehne dena. Teesra: poochna.

Sabse bada teesra tha.

Yellow casserole kisne khareeda tha, usse yaad nahi. Wedding gift ke do serving bowls the, lekin set alag karne mein use ajeeb lag raha tha. Phir khud par gussa aaya: bowls hi toh hain. Agar dono bowls Aarav rakh lega toh uske paas dal daalne ko pan hai.

"Ek saucepan le loongi," usne kaha.

Aarav kitchen mein water bottle bhar raha tha. "Kaunsa?"

"Handle wala steel."

"Haan."

"Tumhe chahiye toh—"

"Meera. Le lo."

Woh chup ho gayi. Usne notebook mein saucepan likha.

Aarav bottle lekar aaya. "Kal society office ko mail kar doonga. Lift slot ke liye."

"Okay."

"Tumhe copy karoon?"

"Haan."

Woh uske laptop ke paas khada tha. Meera ne ek moment ke liye socha woh kuch aur bolega. Naina ke voice note mein jo joke tha, woh bataye? Publisher ka ridiculous correction? Pehle woh aise incidents dinner par suna deti thi. Aarav kabhi genuinely hasta, kabhi bina samjhe haan karta, kabhi poochta client iske paise extra dega?

Aaj use pehle se guess karna tha ki kis tarah ka reply milega. Us guess se thakan hui.

"Mera proof finally gaya," usne kaha.

"Good."

Woh ruka. Phir poocha, "Wahi train wali book?"

"Nahi. School anthology."

"Oh."

Train wali book February mein khatam hui thi.

Meera ne notebook band nahi ki. Uski ungli saucepan ke neeche rahi. Usse chhota sa achha laga ki usne poocha. Saath hi bura laga ki use yaad nahi tha. Dono feelings itni jaldi aayi ki woh kisi ek ko answer bana nahi paayi.

"Ek author ne apna intro third person se first person kar diya," usne kaha. "Ab book mein 'Meera Sane believes' ke beech suddenly 'I was born' aa raha hai."

Aarav hansa. "Plot twist."

"Non-fiction hai."

"Aur bhi bada twist."

Meera ne pen rakha. Woh sach mein hansi, phir bina wajah thoda embarrassed hui. Aarav bottle ka cap khol kar paani peene laga.

"Main baaki shelves kal—" usne shuru kiya.

"Aaj bas ek box karte hain. Heavy books neeche."

"Theek."

Usne folded newspaper ki ek dry sheet li. Aarav shelf ke neeche baith gaya. Book nikaalte waqt ek chhota photo frame peeche se gira aur wooden floor par seedha face down laga.

Meera ka haath usse pehle badha. Glass nahi toota tha. Usne frame uthaya. Peeche stand ki cardboard strip nikal gayi thi.

"Ruko," Aarav ne kaha. "Main dekh leta hoon."

"Glass theek hai."

Usne photograph palti.

Pehle use orange tarpaulin dikhi. Phir Aarav ka chehra. Bahut patla nahi, bas jaw thoda alag. Uske khud ke baal shoulders se upar the. Haath mein paper plate thi, jisme samosa khul kar geela ho gaya tha.

"Yeh yahan tha?" usne kaha.

Aarav uske paas floor par baith gaya.

"Library wala?"

Meera ne frame ki dhool ungli se hata di.

"Naina ne li thi."

Usse us din ki ek awaaz achanak bilkul clear yaad aayi. Kisi ne peeche se chillaya tha ki dictionaries ko alag rakho. Aarav ne usse poocha tha, teen box dictionaries ke kaun khareedega?

Usne jawab diya tha, aap prices laga do, buyers main dekh loongi.

Woh abhi bhi use aap kehti thi.
