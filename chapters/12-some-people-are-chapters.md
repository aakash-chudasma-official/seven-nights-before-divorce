# Chapter 12 — Some People Are Chapters, Not the Whole Book

March ke doosre Saturday Meera apne bag mein ek empty folder daal kar nikli.

Tax papers Aarav ke paas reh gaye the. Copies available thi, lekin original certificates aur old statements alag rakhne the. Usne last week message kiya tha. Aarav ne scans bheje. Phir poocha originals kab de doon? Meera ne library ke afternoon event se pehle cafe suggest kiya. Dono ko neighbourhood convenient tha. Koi coincidence nahi thi. Calendar invite bhi tha, is baar usne bheja tha.

Subah nikalne se pehle usne session ki book kholi. Woh aaj facilitate nahi kar rahi thi. Ek independent publisher ka short-fiction reading tha. Farah ne poster bheja tha. Meera ne attend karne ka decide kiya kyunki editor bhi aa rahi thi. Work ho sakta tha. Nahi bhi. Usne resume print nahi kiya. Phone par portfolio link tha.

Mirror mein apna face dekhkar usne kurta change karna chaha. Green pehna tha; bahut faded tha. White wala better hota. Phir laga white travel mein crease hoga. Phir khud se kaha, kurta choose kar rahi ho ya interview?

Usne green hi pehna. Earrings change ki. Ek ka lock loose tha, doosri pair li. Isme fifteen minutes chale gaye. Woh ab bhi ordinary tarah se late ho sakti thi.

Birthday par Ma aur Naina ke saath lunch hua tha. Naina fifteen minutes late aayi kyunki school ka event extend ho gaya. Meera ko wait karte hue ek moment ko old restaurant yaad aaya. Usne menu khola aur starters order kiye. Naina ne delay ka message pehle bhej diya tha. Same feeling ki shuruat same situation nahi thi. Meera ko yeh consciously yaad karna pada. Lunch achha tha. Ek dessert average. Ma ne bill ka half pay karne ke liye insist kiya; unhone split kar liya. Aarav ne birthday morning message kiya tha, simple wish. Usne thanks kaha. Card nahi aayi. Usne cupboard aur doorbell ko wait karte hue pakda, phir apne aap par thodi naram hui. Kisi old gesture ka na aana bhi feel ho sakta tha, chahe use maanga na ho.

Aaj uske phone par Aarav ka message tha: reached. Inside, left table.

Woh four minutes door thi. Usne reply kiya four minutes. Phir footpath par construction dikha. Usne add kiya maybe seven. Aarav ne okay bheja.

Cafe mein ghuste hi use woh dikh gaya. Blue shirt, sleeves fold. Baal thode chhote. Table par paper folder aur water glass. Uska phone face down tha. Meera ne bag ka strap apne shoulder se hataaya aur andar aayi.

"Hi," usne kaha.

Aarav khada hua. "Hi."

Hug nahi hua. Woh seat par baith gayi. Bag next chair par rakha. Aarav ne menu uski taraf kiya.

"Coffee?"

"Haan. Kuch khaaya?"

"Breakfast. Tum?"

"Thoda. Sandwich le loongi."

Unhone order diya. Usne apni coffee choose ki, Aarav ne apni. Meera ko mushroom toast ka naam dekhkar ek purana dinner yaad aaya. Usne menu band kar diya. Har memory ko table par laana zaroori nahi tha.

Aarav ne folder uski taraf sarakaya. "Yeh originals. Two statements ka duplicate print hai. Upar sticky note."

"Thanks."

Usne pages check kiye. Aarav neeche receipt ka date explain karne laga. Meera ne kaha samajh aa gaya. Usne stop kiya. Phir usne ek statement dekha jisme spelling wrong thi. "Yeh mere old surname ke saath—"

"Bank wala," Aarav ne kaha. "Maine scan alag bheja."

"Okay. Update poochna padega."

"Haan."

Meera ne papers apne empty folder mein rakhe. Ek page missing tha. Usne poocha toh Aarav ne phone par scan dekha. Original uske tax file mein tha. Woh laana bhool gaya tha.

Usne face rub kiya. "Sorry. Specifically rakha tha. Phir—"

"Courier kar dena."

"Main drop—"

"Courier enough."

"Okay. Monday."

Woh sheet ka naam note karne laga. Meera ne paani piya. Galti hui thi. Woh shayad phir karega. Use har forgotten paper mein apna forgotten hona dhoondhna nahi tha. Phir bhi ek second ke liye woh impulse aaya tha. Usne use dekha aur jaane diya.

Coffee aa gayi. Aarav ne sugar packet nahi liya. Meera ne half li. Table ke beech extra packets ka pile tha. Usne packet fold karke plate ke edge par rakha. Aarav ne sandwich ka poocha kaisa hai. Usne bite lekar kaha abhi garam hai, review pending.

Woh hansa.

***

Aarav ko Meera ko apne saamne baithkar khana khate dekhna unsettling lag raha tha.

Uske kurte ka sleeve fold baar-baar khul raha tha. Woh left hand se use adjust karti, right se sandwich pakadti. Yeh gesture woh jaanta tha. Bag par nayi keychain thi. Woh nahi jaanta tha. Usne dono ko ek saath notice kiya.

"Curtains shorten hui?" usne poocha.

Meera ne uski taraf dekha, phir hansi. "Haan. Last week."

"Good."

"Tumne table bechi?"

"Nahi. Fit kar li. Ek chair wall mein ghusti hai."

"Photo bheji thi. Achha lag raha tha."

"Photo wide angle thi."

Meera ki hansi familiar thi, lekin woh uske liye perform nahi kar rahi thi. Aarav ne coffee uthayi. Usne socha poochhe kaam kaisa. Generic answer aa sakta tha. Phir bhi usne poocha.

Meera ne retainer khatam hone ka bataya. Usse nahi pata tha. Uska immediate response number calculate karne ka hua: rent, buffer, income. Usne poocha, "Abhi manage ho raha?"

"Ho raha. Do short projects mile. Stable nahi."

"Main agar—"

Woh ruk gaya. Meera usse dekh rahi thi.

"Contacts pooch sakta hoon," usne kaha. "Paise dene wala sentence nahi. Kabir ke cousin ka education business hai. Unhe editing chahiye ho sakti."

Meera ne thoda socha. "Contact bhej dena. Promise mat karna meri taraf se. Scope dekhkar boloongi."

"Haan."

"Aur tumhara training?"

Aarav ne cup rakha. "Same programme nahi. Ek shorter course kar raha. Company sponsor nahi."

"Achha?"

"Tuesday evening. Week mein ek class. Pehli assignment mein numbers galat daale."

"Tum?"

"Meri reputation bachne do."

Woh hansi. Aarav ne bhi. Usne bataya assignment dobara submit ki thi, grade better hua. Class mein ek twenty-six-year-old usse spreadsheet ke shortcut sikhata. Pehle irritate hua, phir usse help maangi. Ab woh dono project group mein the. Kaam ka workload kam nahi hua tha. Tuesday ko dinner late hota. Kabhi class skip karne ka mann karta.

Meera ne kaha, "Recordings milti?"

"Milti. Isi liye skip karna easy. Phir recordings dekhte nahi."

"Classic."

Usne use counsellor ki baat nahi batayi jo usne November mein start ki thi. Secret nahi tha, lekin woh har current effort ko Meera ke saamne evidence nahi banana chahta tha. Kabir ko pata tha. Apni life mein koi ek cheez uske response ke bina bhi kar sakta tha.

Phone vibrate hua. Papa ka call. Usne screen dekhi. Meera ne kaha uthao. Usne call liya. Papa ne courier ka tracking number poocha. Aarav ne kaha ghar aakar bhejta hoon. Unhone poocha meeting mein hai? Aarav ne Meera ki taraf dekha, phir kaha, "Meera se mil raha. Papers dene the."

Udhar brief silence hua. Phir Papa ne kaha, "Usse namaste."

Usne pass kiya. Meera ne phone nahi liya, bas loud enough namaste kaha. Papa ne poocha theek? Usne haan kaha. Bas. Call band.

Aarav ne phone table par rakha. "Ab Ma ko batayenge."

Meera ne cup ka handle rotate kiya. "Phir Ma meri Ma ko."

"Full network."

Woh dono hase. Phir Meera ne seedha poocha, "Tumhe explain karna padega?"

"Poochenge. Bol doonga papers the. Aur coffee."

"Haan. Coffee chhupane ki zaroorat nahi."

Aarav ne uski taraf dekha. Usne ek chhota nod diya. Yeh permission ya declaration nahi thi. Woh bas uske saamne apni present choice ko chhota nahi karna chahti thi.

Unke papers ka process ab bhi chal raha tha. Agli appointment ki date decide karni thi. Aarav ne diary kholi. Meera ne apna calendar. Dono ne availability bhejne ka decide kiya. Kisi ne appointment postpone karne ki baat nahi ki. Aarav ko isse sadness hui. Saath hi strange relief ki woh unke beech hidden bargaining chip nahi ban gayi.

Meera ne sandwich ka last corner plate mein chhod diya. "Bread dry hai," usne kaha.

"Review aa gaya."

"Two stars. Coffee four."

"Average three."

"Tum sabka average nikaal dete ho."

Woh ruk gaya. Meera ne uski taraf dekha, phir smile ki. "Joke."

Usne haan kaha. Uska body ek moment ko defensive ho gaya tha. Usne cup ko table par rakha aur shoulders dheele kiye. Woh badal gaya ho, iski uniform proof nahi thi. Woh bas ab apni first reaction ke baad kabhi doosra second paa leta tha.

***

Cafe ke bahar sunlight tez thi. Meera ne phone mein event ka time dekha. Two-thirty. Ab two bajne wale the. Walk fifteen minutes ki thi. Woh decide kar rahi thi nikalna hai ya Aarav se baat aur karni hai.

"Tum reading mein jaa rahe?" usne poocha.

"Poster dekha tha. Socha agar seats ho."

"Registration free hai. Wahaan naam likh sakte."

Usne haan kaha. Meera ko pata tha woh aa sakta tha; usne event pehle mention kiya tha. Phir bhi dono ka saath jaana automatically fixed nahi tha. Usne poocha, "Saath chalein?"

"Haan."

Bill aaya toh Aarav ne poora pay karne ko haath badhaya. Meera ne apna phone khola. Usne kaha half? Meera ne kaha mine, tumhari black coffee cheap thi. Aarav hansa. Unhone separate amounts pay kiye. Waiter ko do payments track karni padi. Meera ne thank you kaha. Isme koi moral statement nahi tha; bas ab unke kharche alag the.

Footpath ke pehle section par pavement broken thi. Aarav naturally road side chalne laga. Meera ne uski movement notice ki. Woh pehle bhi aisa karta tha. Usne us care ko isliye reject nahi kiya ki shaadi mein usse aur cheezein nahi mili. Usse achha laga. Woh road cross karte hue khud signal dekh rahi thi.

Library tak unhone weather aur route par baat ki. Phir ek bookstore ke shutter ke saamne ruk gaye. Notice tha inventory ke liye closed. Meera ne window se andar ki shelves dekhi. Aarav bhi ruka.

"Tumhari shop ka kya?" usne poocha.

"Abhi shop nahi. Sessions. Paid editing. Buffer. Baad mein dekhoongi."

"Naam?"

"Wahi notebook hai."

"After Hours?"

"Ho sakta. Ho sakta bilkul kuch aur."

Aarav ne haan kaha. Usne rent estimate nahi poocha. Meera ne khud add kiya, "Numbers phir banaoongi. Is baar deposit bhi."

Woh hasa. "Good start."

Usne use elbow se halka touch kiya. Bas mazaak mein. Touch ke baad dono ek moment chup rahe. Meera ne apna haath bag par rakh liya. Footpath phir chalne ke liye jagah de rahi thi.

"Aaj achha lag raha," Aarav ne kaha.

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. "Mujhe bhi."

Woh library se do buildings door the. Entry ke paas teen log poster padh rahe the. Ek woman apne phone par kisi ko directions de rahi thi. Meera ko andar pahunchkar normal people ke beech dissolve ho jaana achha lagta. Abhi unke beech woh incomplete question wapas aa raha tha.

Aarav ne poocha, "Hum phir mil sakte hain? Papers ke bina."

Uska throat tight hua. Yeh question use achha laga. Darr bhi laga. Agar haan kaha toh family expectations, agar nahi kaha toh apni curiosity ko band kar degi. Usne apne answer ko sirf aaj ka size dene ki koshish ki.

"Haan," usne kaha. "Par main abhi wapas saath rehne ki baat nahi kar rahi."

"Main bhi abhi woh nahi pooch raha."

"Aage kya hoga, mujhe nahi pata."

"Mujhe bhi."

Woh dono gate ke side khade ho gaye taaki entry block na ho. Aarav ne apni fingers pocket ke edge par rakhi. Meera ko yaad aaya jab nervous hota tab aise karta tha. Pehle usse lagta tha bas impatient hai. Kabhi shayad impatient bhi hota hoga. Woh har past gesture ki correct explanation ab nahi dhoondh sakti thi.

"Kya yeh date hoga?" Aarav ne poocha.

Meera ne halka sa saans li. "Mujhe label se pressure aa raha."

"Okay."

"Coffee ho sakti hai. Aur honestly bata sakte hain agar kisi point par nahi chahiye. Bina disappear hue."

Usne sir hilaaya. "Haan."

"Main bhi."

Aarav ne uski taraf dekha. "Tum yeh isliye nahi kar rahi ki mujhe achha lage?"

Meera ne apne answer ko check kiya. Aadat thi. Care bhi. Curiosity bhi. Aur ek purani affection jo months ke baad bhi exact naam nahi leti thi.

"Nahi," usne kaha. "Tumse baat karna chahti hoon. Kabhi-kabhi. Abhi itna clear hai."

"Mere liye bhi."

Usne bag ka strap adjust kiya. "Next week? Main schedule bhejoongi. Abhi Thursday free lag raha, par assignment confirm—"

"Schedule bhej dena."

Woh smile ki. "Haan. Agar change hua toh pehle bol doongi."

Koi promise nahi ki ab kabhi delay nahi hoga. Koi poori zindagi ka timetable nahi. Meera ne dekha gate par Farah aa gayi thi. Usne door se hello kiya. Farah ne kaha andar seats le lo, author early aa gayi. Aarav ne gate khola aur side hua. Meera andar chali.

Library corridor mein paper aur floor cleaner ki smell thi. Building unki first meeting wali nahi thi. Same city, different room. Meera ne registration sheet par naam likha. Profession ke column mein editor. Aarav ne operations likha, phir sign kiya. Volunteer badge nahi tha. Cash box nahi. Koi box uthaane ki zaroorat nahi thi.

Inside, chairs ki teen rows thi. Front mein ek folding table, jug aur microphone. Author apne pages arrange kar rahi thi. Usne stage par koi dramatic entry nahi li. Woh spectacles dhoondh rahi thi jo uske head par the. Audience mein kisi ne point kiya. Woh hansi aur glasses neeche kiye.

Meera ne middle row ki end chair choose ki. Aarav ne uske paas poocha, "Yahan?"

"Haan."

Woh baith gaya. Usne bag floor par rakha. Meera ne empty chair ke back se apna bag uthaya aur pair ke paas rakh liya. Ab dono ke paas apni seat thi. Room bharne laga. Ek older man ne unse poocha last chair free? Aarav ne kaha haan, aap le lijiye. Woh chair khiska kar apne dost ke paas le gaya. Chhoti rearrangement ke baad sab phir settle ho gaye.

Farah ne welcome kaha. Meera ne apna phone silent kiya. Usne Aarav ki taraf dekha; usne bhi kiya. Phir uske phone par screen lit hui. Papa ka message: tracking mil gaya. Aarav ne read kiya, reply baad ke liye chhoda aur phone bag mein rakh diya.

Meera ke paas ek printed programme tha. Usne extra copy Aarav ko di. Woh titles dekhne laga. Ek story ka title tha *The House on the Other Side*. Meera ne socha is par joke kare, phir author bolne lagi. Usne programme lap par rakh diya.

Reading ki first story mein ek old woman apna fridge bech rahi thi. Buyer price kam kar raha tha. Meera ko kitchen ka saucepan yaad aaya, phir brass strainer, phir first Sunday ka library email. Memories aayi aur scene sunte hue chali gayi. Author ke next sentence par audience hansi. Aarav bhi hasa. Meera thoda late hansi kyunki uska dhyaan abhi return hua tha.

Pause mein usne paani ki bottle kholi. Cap floor par gir gaya. Aarav ne jhuk kar uthaya aur diya. Usne thank you kaha. Bottle se sip li. Programme par ek small water mark reh gaya.

Usne phone check nahi kiya. Kal ka invoice, Monday ki missing sheet, next week ki possible coffee, sab outside room mein available the. Yahan usse bas next line sunni thi.

Author ne page palta. Fan ki hawa se corner utha toh usne palm rakh di aur reading continue ki.

Meera apni chair par thoda seedhi baithi. Uske baju mein Aarav ne programme fold karke pocket mein rakha.

Phir dono kahaani sunne lage.
