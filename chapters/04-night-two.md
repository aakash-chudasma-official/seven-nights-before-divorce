# Chapter 4 — Night Two: The Things We Never Said

Friday ko Aarav ne lift reservation ka mail eleven-seventeen par bheja.

Meera ko copy kiya. Society office ne eleven-twenty par reply diya ki service lift ki maintenance scheduled hai, normal lift ke liye protective covers lagwaane padenge. Aarav ne charges pooche. Office ne quotation bheja. Usne approve kiya, phir Meera ko alag message likha: done.

Reply mein thumbs-up aaya.

Office mein Kabir uski desk ke paas khada tha. "Bahar chai?"

"Five minutes."

"Tu five minutes bol, phir main calendar invite bhejoon?"

Aarav ne screen lock ki. Baarish ruk gayi thi. Office building ke side mein chai ka counter tha, blue plastic roof aur ek permanent geeli bench ke saath. Kabir ne bench ki jagah khade rehna choose kiya. Uski shirt par collar ke neeche chhota toothpaste mark tha.

"School drop?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Haan. Beti ne bola aap brush nahi karte kya. Main bola tere saamne hi kiya tha."

Chai wale ne unhe bina pooche do cups diye. Ek mein chini kam. Aarav ne apna cup uthaya.

Kabir kuch der traffic dekhta raha. Phir bola, "Kal wali baat... main kisi ko nahi bata raha."

"Thanks."

"HR ko kabhi leave ke liye bolna ho toh alag."

"Leave nahi chahiye. Thursday half day le loonga."

"Full le le. Shifting hai."

"Uski shifting hai."

Sentence nikalne ke baad Aarav ko uska sound achha nahi laga. Kabir ne cup neeche kiya.

"Haan," Aarav ne kaha. "Full le leta hoon."

Kabir ne phone check kiya. Wife ka message tha. Usne turant reply nahi kiya. Pocket mein rakh diya. Phir nikaal kar kuch type kiya.

"Kal groceries meri thi," usne kaha, jaise explain karna ho. "Bread bhool gaya. Ab lecture aayega."

Aarav ne almost kaha, same everywhere. Usne chai pi li.

Kabir ne khud add kiya, "Fair bhi hai. Teen baar bola tha."

Unke peeche koi vendor phone par loud speaker mein rate discuss kar raha tha. Aarav ko office wapas jaane ki jaldi hui. Phir yaad aaya woh khud hi kal chai ke liye haan bola tha, ya almost haan.

"Kya bolte ho ghar par?" Kabir ne poocha.

"Kis baare mein?"

"Abhi. Matlab everyday."

"Packing. Documents. Aise."

"Okay."

"Kal photo mili. Purani. Library wali."

Kabir hansa. "Jahan tu volunteer karne aaya tha aur do hafte baad shave karke date par gaya."

"Main shave karta tha."

"Every fiscal quarter."

Aarav hansa. Us hansi ke baad chest mein kuch tight hua. Usne chai ka empty cup dustbin mein rakha.

"Ajeeb hai," usne kaha.

Kabir ne poocha nahi ki kya. Bas haan kaha. Is baar Aarav ko uski yeh kami achhi lagi.

Office mein ek client ka dispatch problem tha. Lunch ke baad problem solve hui, phir doosre warehouse se quantity mismatch aaya. Aarav ne do junior staff ko kaam diya aur khud third tab khola. Three-thirty par usse yaad aaya ki usne Meera ko lunch ke liye kabhi message nahi kiya. Usne phone uthaya.

> Aaj dinner order kar doon? Tum packing kar lena.

Usne send kiya. Isme koi risk nahi lag raha tha. Food predictable tha. Restaurant, delivery, bill.

Meera ka reply twenty minutes baad aaya.

> Okay. Main 8 tak kaam karoongi.

Usne unka purana restaurant khola. Family favourites wale section mein saved order tha: paneer lababdar, butter naan, mushroom pulao. Usne quantity adjust ki. Payment ki. Delivery seven-forty-five.

Usse achha laga ki aaj kuch pehle ho gaya.

***

Meera ko naye flat mein apna desk kahan rakhna hai, us par forty minutes waste ho gaye.

Usne floor plan par chair ki jagah banayi. Window ke paas rakhe toh afternoon mein screen par glare aayega. Andar rakhe toh wall hi dekhegi. Current tenant se poocha ki plug point ka photo bhej de. Usne teen photos bheje, teeno fan ke.

Naina ne phone kiya toh Meera ne kaha, "Main ceiling edit karne jaa rahi hoon. Bahut achha view hai."

Naina has padi. "Landlord ko bol painter ke saath electrician bhi bhej de."

"Har request par woh 'we'll see' bolta hai."

"Written le."

"Le rahi hoon."

Naina ke office mein koi usse pooch raha tha ki blue jacket wali copy kahan hai. Usne phone se hat kar kaha pantry mein nahi hogi, pehle desk dekho.

"Tu abhi bhi wahan hai?" Meera ne poocha.

"Haan. Ek author ko apna title subah four baje galat lagne laga. Ab sab jag rahe hain."

Meera ne copy ke rates par baat karni chahi. Naina ne kaha usse ek short assignment yaad aaya hai, introductions ka cleanup, zyada paise nahi. Meera ne turant haan kehne ke bajaye details bhejne ko kaha.

"Tu dekh le," Naina ne kaha. "Mana bhi kar sakti hai."

"Pata hai."

Phir Naina ne poocha ghar par sab theek hai na. Meera ne kaha haan, as theek as possible.

"Zyada normal behave kare toh confuse mat ho," Naina ne kaha.

Meera ne notebook se pen uthaya. "Kya?"

"Bas. Kabhi log end par achhe ho jaate hain."

"Woh villain nahi hai, Naina."

Udhar silence hua, phir Naina boli, "Maine kab kaha?"

"Tumhare bolne ka... chhodo."

"Main teri side par hoon."

"Pata hai. Har cheez ki side nahi hoti."

Yeh sentence bolte hi Meera ko khud par gussa aaya. Kal tak woh isi friend ko bata rahi thi ki Aarav kitni baar nahi sunta. Aaj usne usse protect kyun kiya? Shayad Naina ka sentence convenient tha. Shayad incomplete. Dono ho sakta tha.

"Sorry," usne kaha. "Bas aaj tired hoon."

Naina ne bhi sorry kaha. Phir introductions wali file bhejne ka promise kiya. Call khatam hua toh Meera ne phone table ke doosre end par rakha.

Retainer client ne ek sentence par poocha tha, does this sound too stiff? Meera ne usse do alternatives diye. Dono ke neeche reasons likhe. Apni life ke liye woh ek reply draft nahi kar pa rahi thi, aur stranger ke paragraph ko natural banane mein fifteen minutes laga rahi thi.

Eight baje doorbell hui.

Delivery packets Aarav ne liye. Usne dining table se laptop ke cable hata kar jagah banayi. Meera ne file save ki aur kitchen mein plates lene gayi. Packet khulte hi mushroom ki smell aayi.

"Mushroom pulao?"

"Haan."

"Okay. Main plain rice kar leti hoon."

Aarav ne container ki foil se haath hata liya. "Kyun?"

"Mushroom ab heavy lagta hai."

"Tum khati thi."

"Ab nahi khati."

"Kab se?"

Meera ne shelf se chhota pan nikaala. "Kuch months."

"Tumne bataya nahi."

Usne water filter ka tap khola. "Ab bata diya."

Woh use dekh raha tha. Meera ne pan gas par rakha. "Itna issue nahi hai. Main rice bana loongi. Ten minutes."

"Maine order hi isliye kiya ki tumhe banana na pade."

"Haan. Thanks."

Usne rice measure kiya. Quarter cup kaafi tha. Kabhi-kabhi mushroom khane ke baad use bloating hoti thi. Usne ek dinner par Aarav ko bataya tha, ya shayad Naina ko? Clear nahi tha. Phir bhi uske 'tumne bataya nahi' mein ek file-return jaisi feeling aayi: information not provided, request cannot be processed.

"Main plain rice order kar deta hoon," Aarav ne kaha.

"Ab paani chadha diya."

"Cancel kar sakti ho."

"Kyun?"

"Because main order kar raha hoon."

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. "Rice hi toh hai. Mujhe cooking se allergy nahi."

Aarav ne foil fold kar di. Uska jaw tight ho gaya. Meera ne notice kiya aur nazar hata li. Abhi bas chup rahein toh dinner nikal sakta tha.

Woh salad ka dabba kholne laga. "Naan khayogi?"

"Ek."

"Pehle do khati thi."

"Ab iska bhi update doon?"

Usne bolte hi pan ka handle doosri taraf kiya. Aarav ne dabba rakh diya.

"Main count nahi kar raha tha."

"Toh mat karo."

"Meera."

Gas ke paas hawa garam thi. Usne flame kam ki. Aarav door se nahi, bilkul paas khada tha; phir bhi usse lag raha tha woh stage ke doosre corner par uske saamne defend kar rahi hai.

"Maine sirf dinner mangaya hai," usne kaha.

"Maine sirf rice banaya hai."

"Har cheez aise kyun ho jaati hai?"

Meera hansi nahi, par uske muh se ek dry awaaz nikli. "Ab yeh bhi main bataoon?"

Aarav ne phone uthaya. Woh usse dekhkar samajh nahi paayi ki order kar raha hai ya conversation se nikal raha hai.

"Phone rakh sakte ho do minute?"

"Rice order kar raha tha."

"Maine mana kiya na."

Usne screen band ki. "Theek. Kuch nahi karta."

Meera ko woh sentence bahut purana laga. Jab woh exactly kisi cheez ke liye kehti, uske jawab mein sab kuch band ho jaata. Woh explain karne lagi, "Maine yeh nahi—"

"Pata hai. Tumne sab kuch karne se mana nahi kiya. Sirf jo main kar raha hoon, woh wrong hai."

"Maine wrong kaha?"

"Kehna padta hai?"

Pan mein paani bubble kar raha tha. Meera ne lid lagaya. Metal ka edge uski finger se touch hua, garam nahi tha phir bhi usne haath jhatka.

"Tumhe ek dinner mein itna effort lag raha hai," usne kaha, "main roz sochti thi kya banega, kya khatam hai, kisko kab khana hai. Tab tumhe normal lagta tha."

"Main groceries laata tha."

"List main banati thi."

"Toh main kuch karta hi nahi tha?"

"Yeh tumne kaha."

Usne seedha uski taraf dekha. "Tum bolti nahi, bas aise arrange karti ho ki end mein yahi nikle."

Meera ke haath thande ho gaye. Woh uske sentence mein apna ek sach pehchaan rahi thi aur usse nafrat hui ki woh iss waqt sahi keh raha tha.

"Kam se kam maine ghar ko hotel nahi samjha," usne kaha.

Aarav ki aankhein ek moment ke liye badli. Gussa kam, kuch aur. Meera use dekhna nahi chahti thi.

"Hotel," usne repeat kiya.

Usne jawab nahi diya.

"Rent, bills, tumhari Ma ke appointments par drive, raat ko medicine—hotel."

"Unka bill tumse kisne maanga?"

"Main bill nahi—"

"Abhi toh list bana rahe ho."

"Kyunki tumne jo bola—"

"Haan, galat bola. Khush?"

Woh peeche hata. "Nahi. Khush nahi."

Meera ne lid uthaya. Steam uske face par aayi. Rice ko abhi time tha. Woh doosri taraf mud gayi, kyunki use aankhon mein paani aate hue dikhana ek aur argument lag raha tha.

Aarav ne dheere kaha, "Kabhi lagta hai tumhe meri zaroorat sirf tab hoti thi jab kuch fix karna ho. Baaki main bas disturb kar raha tha."

"Tum fix karne ke alawa hote kahan the?"

Uska jawab ready aaya. "Tumhare saath. Isi ghar mein."

"Laptop ke peeche."

Usne ab kuch nahi kaha. Us silence se Meera aur bol sakti thi. Uske paas examples the. Christmas dinner. Dentist ka wait. Ek Sunday jab woh poora din ghar par the aur usne raat ko poocha tha aaj ka din kaisa tha, jaise woh alag cities mein ho.

Usne koi example nahi diya. Pan ka paani dry hone laga tha.

"Tum khao," usne kaha.

Aarav dining table par gaya. Usne plate mein thoda paneer aur naan rakha. Meera ko chair ki awaaz aayi. Woh sink ke saamne khadi rahi, haath ke saamne clean sponge tha, phir bhi koi bartan nahi dho rahi thi.

***

Paneer thanda ho raha tha. Aarav ne ek bite liya; gravy mein namak zyada tha. Ya uska mouth dry tha.

Hotel.

Usse har woh kaam yaad aa raha tha jo Meera ko shayad normal lagta tha. Water purifier ka filter. Parents ke insurance renewals. Landlord ko late-night leak ka call. Uske laptop ki hard disk fail hui thi toh Saturday bhar service centre. Woh list uske dimaag mein apne aap ban rahi thi. Usne use rokne ki koshish nahi ki.

Kitchen se rice ki smell aayi. Meera ne gas band ki. Spoon se grains dekhe, lid rakha, phir bathroom chali gayi.

Aarav ne phone khola. Delivery app par order repeat ka option tha. Usne dekha saved favourite ke neeche last ordered eleven months ago likha tha.

Usne favourites mein se mushroom pulao hata diya. Phir phone rakh diya. Isse kuch solve nahi hua.

Meera bathroom se aayi toh face dhula hua tha. Usne apna rice liya, thoda paneer, aur table ke doosre side baith gayi. Naan nahi liya. Aarav ne packet uski taraf kiya; usne head shake kiya.

Woh kuch der sirf khate rahe. Clock ke second hand ki awaaz kitchen ke exhaust se cut rahi thi. Meera ne uth kar exhaust band kiya. Wapas aakar usne rice mein gravy mix ki.

"Hotel nahi kehna chahiye tha," usne kaha.

Aarav ne bite nigla. "Haan."

"Mujhe pata hai tum kaam karte the. Ghar ke liye bhi."

Uske andar turant aaya: phir bolti kyun ho. Usne paani piya.

Meera ne add kiya, "Main us baat se peeche nahi hat rahi ki mujhe akela lagta tha. Dono alag—"

"Pata hai," Aarav ne kaha. Thoda tez.

Woh chup ho gayi. Usse phir bura laga, lekin abhi woh har sentence ke sahi speed par react nahi kar paa raha tha.

"Mujhe thoda time do," usne kaha.

Meera ne fork plate mein rakh diya. Woh use dekh rahi thi.

"Abhi," Aarav ne kaha. "Main dinner finish kar leta hoon. Phir..."

Usne sentence poora nahi kiya. Kya phir? Baith kar poori shaadi discuss karega? Kal raat ek photo par hansne se aaj woh incapable kaise ho gaya tha?

"Theek," Meera ne kaha.

Woh khana khatam karne laga. Pulao ab half se zyada bacha tha. Usne socha kal office le jayega, phir yaad aaya Saturday tha. Fridge mein rakh dega.

Meera ne apni plate sink mein rakhi aur work chair par baith gayi. Usne laptop nahi khola. Phone par kuch padhne lagi. Aarav ne containers ek saath kiye. Plain rice ke liye usne alag dabba nikala. Jo lid uske haath aayi woh fit nahi thi. Doosri fit thi. Usne dhoondh li.

Kitchen counter par Meera ka saucepan reh gaya tha. Neeche thin rice ki layer chipak gayi thi. Aarav ne usme paani bhara. Usse yaad aaya pichhle week usne ek aisa hi pan sink mein rakh diya tha aur kaha tha soak kar raha hoon. Do din baad Meera ne saaf kiya tha. Us waqt usne genuinely socha tha paani bhar dena uska part tha, kisi ne bhi baad mein dho diya toh kya.

Aaj usse apna woh thought petty laga. Saath hi use yeh bhi laga ki woh ek pan ke liye khud ko cross-examine kar raha hai kyunki Meera ne usse hotel guest kaha.

Dono thoughts ek saath the. Woh unhe sort nahi kar saka.

Usne pan stove par rakha, thoda paani garam kiya aur wooden spoon se layer dheere uthayi. Achha khaasa time laga. Counter par paani gira. Usne cloth liya. Meera kitchen mein aayi toh usne aankhein uthayi, jaise pakda gaya ho.

"Hot hai," usne kaha. "Abhi mat chhoona."

Meera ne cupboard se biscuit ka packet liya. Wapas jaate hue ruk gayi.

"Maine mushroom wali baat shayad batayi hi nahi thi," usne kaha.

Aarav ne tap band kiya.

"Mujhe yaad nahi," usne add kiya. "Naina ko bola tha. Tumhe... sure nahi hoon."

Usse relief hua. Phir us relief par gussa aaya, jaise woh trial mein ek chhota point jeet kar khush ho raha ho.

"Okay," usne kaha.

"Bas clear karna tha."

Woh jaane lagi. Aarav ne kaha, "Main pehle pooch sakta tha. Order se pehle."

"Haan."

"Saved tha. Bas wahi mangaa diya."

Meera ne packet fold kiya. "Mujhe idea achha laga tha. Jab message aaya."

Woh uski taraf dekhne laga.

"Order karne ka," usne kaha. "Usse pehle ki maine sab kharab kar diya."

"Maine bhi kiya."

Usne yeh instinct se kaha, fairness maintain karne ke liye. Phir usse laga usko specific hona chahiye. Lekin woh abhi nahi kar paa raha tha. Words dimaag mein formal lag rahe the: I invalidated. I reacted. Jaise HR training ka module.

"Jab tumne bola rice bana loongi," usne dheere kaha, "mujhe laga mera karna waste gaya. Tumhe blame kar diya."

Meera ne biscuits counter par rakhe. "Mujhe laga tum chahte ho main thank you bolke bas kha loon."

"Nahi."

Usne turant jawab diya. Phir ruk kar kaha, "Maybe thoda. Thank you toh chahiye tha."

Meera ne nazar neeche ki. Uske muh par smile nahi thi. Aarav usse yeh confession charming lage, aisa nahi chahta tha. Woh khud use bahut achha nahi lag raha tha.

"Main din bhar approval dhoondhta rehta hoon," usne kaha. "Office mein bhi. Ghar aake laga ek dinner toh..."

Sentence aage nahi gaya.

Meera ne paani ka glass uthaya. "Main tumhari appraisal nahi hoon."

"Pata hai."

Uski tone se Aarav ko dard hua, lekin woh line unfair nahi thi. Meera ko shayad yeh pata chala. Usne turant soft karne ke bajaye thoda socha.

"Par main thank you bol sakti hoon," usne kaha. "Bina iska matlab nikale ki phir kabhi complain nahi karoongi."

Aarav ne slow nod kiya.

Usse laga unhe ab hug karna chahiye, ya kam se kam ek hansi. Kuch toh closure. Meera ne biscuit packet uthaya aur kaha, "Mujhe woh twenty-page sample dekhna hai. Naina ne bheja."

"Abhi?"

"Haan. Rates kal dene hain."

Woh apni chair par chali gayi. Aarav kitchen mein khada raha. Pan saaf tha. Usne dry karke shelf par rakhne se pehle handle ki direction dekhi. Jis taraf Meera rakhti thi, wahi rakha. Phir use andar se irritation hui ki ab woh handle ko bhi test samajh raha hai.

Usne kitchen light band ki.

Living room mein photograph ab bhi dictionary par tiki thi. Shaam ka safai wala cloth uske peeche tha. Usne photo ko chhua nahi.

Guest room mein jaakar usne phone par leave request kholi. Thursday ke liye full day select kiya. Reason mein personal likha. Submit karne ke baad usne Meera ko message type kiya ki leave le li.

Send nahi kiya.

Subah bata dega. Abhi use usse ek aur good job nahi maangna tha.
