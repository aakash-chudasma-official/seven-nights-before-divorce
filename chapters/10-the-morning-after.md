# Chapter 10 — The Morning After

Aarav ki aankh alarm se pehle khuli.

Seven-ten. Fan chal raha tha. Door ki line mein morning light thi. Usne kuch seconds tak date yaad nahi ki. Phir living room se tape ki awaaz aayi.

Woh uthkar bahar aaya. Meera ek box par KITCHEN likh rahi thi. Uske baal wet the. Side mein ek towel tha jo suitcase close hone ke baad yaad aaya hoga. Aarav ne good morning kaha. Usne bhi kaha. Dono ki awaaz thodi heavy thi, jaise raat mein cold ho gaya ho.

"Chai?" usne poocha.

"Bana di. Gas par."

Usne kitchen mein saucepan dekha. Meera wala. Chai mein adrak thi. Usne do cups nikaale; blue chipped mug sink ke paas toothbrush ke saath tha. Uske paas ab bhi normal mugs the. Usne cupboard se white cup liya. Meera ne apna steel cup pehle hi bag se nikaal liya tha.

"Cup le jaa rahi ho?" usne poocha.

"Haan. Last mein daal doongi."

"Okay."

Usne chai pour ki. Half saucepan mein chhodi; shayad mover ko chahiye hogi. Phir socha two people aur honge toh fresh bana lega. Har task ka exact forecast zaroori nahi.

Breakfast ke liye bread tha. Butter ka packet hard tha. Meera ne toast seedha khaaya. Aarav ne poocha egg nahi—phir ruk gaya. Woh khud kabhi-kabhi omelette karta tha, Meera nahi khati thi. Usne aaj koi extra breakfast plan banaya hi nahi tha.

"Neeche se poha le aaoon?" usne kaha.

"Abhi nahi. Cab book karni hai."

"Main laa sakta hoon."

Meera ne clock dekhi. "Theek. Small."

Woh downstairs gaya toh security desk par lift covers nahi aaye the. Guard ne kaha housekeeping aayegi. Aarav ne office ka number call kiya. Supervisor ne kaha ten tak lag jayenge. Usne reminder maanga. Phir poha liya. Vendor ne poocha do packets? Usne haan kaha, phir socha small bola tha. Ek ko do plates mein divide karna chahiye tha. Already pack ho gaya. Usne le liya.

Wapas aakar Meera ne ek packet fridge mein rakh diya. "Tum lunch mein kha lena," usne kaha.

Lunch ka khayal usse pehli baar aaya. Usne kaha haan.

Ten-thirty tak lift covers lag gaye. Movers eleven-fifteen par aayenge. Agent eleven par nayi building mein hoga. Meera ne bag uthaaya, keys check ki aur door ki taraf chali. Aarav ne poocha originals? Usne folder dikhaya.

Woh already door khol rahi thi. Aarav ko ek second ke liye laga bas, yahi goodbye hai. Phir usne kaha, "Main keys lekar wapas nahi aaungi. Tum list ka photo bhej dena, main match kar loongi."

"Haan."

"Aur woh work chair... cover karna."

"Karwa doonga."

Woh uski taraf mud gayi. Bag ka strap shoulder se slip hua. Usne adjust kiya. Aarav doorway ke side khada tha. Unke beech shoes ki ek pair, ek carton aur usual doormat thi.

"Bye," Meera ne kaha, phir face badla. "Matlab abhi..."

"Pata hai."

Uski aankhon mein paani aa gaya. Aarav aage aaya. Is baar hug usne initiate ki. Meera uske shoulders se lagi, bag unke beech atak gaya. Usne bag side kiya. Thoda aur paas aayi. Aarav ke haath uski back par the. Itna familiar ki usse ek second ke liye laga woh office jaa raha hai, woh nahi.

Meera alag hui toh usne poocha, "Text kar doon keys milne par?"

"Haan. Please."

"Okay."

Lift aa gayi. Usne bag uthaaya. Aarav ne usse yaad nahi dilaya ki umbrella reh gaya. Woh uske haath mein tha. Usne bas lift ka button dabaye rakha jab tak woh andar nahi hui.

Door band hone ke baad woh ghar mein aaya. Living room full tha. Meera chali gayi thi, uska samaan abhi nahi. Usse yeh sequence bilkul sahi nahi laga. Phir movers ka call aaya, gate ka code chahiye tha. Usne bataya.

Do aadmi aaye. Ek ne shoes door par utaare, doosre ne poocha utaarne hain? Aarav ne kaha jaisa comfortable. Usne turant add kiya floor wet nahi hai. Aadmi confuse hua, phir shoes utaar diye.

Boxes count hue. Twelve. Plus chair, shelf panels, suitcases. Meera ne estimate mein ten likhe the. Aadmi ne difference poocha. Aarav ne kaha small boxes hain, total load same. Woh team lead se call karne laga. Five-minute discussion ke baad same charge confirm hua. Aarav ka patience ek moment ko khatam hone laga, phir usne Meera ka screenshot dikhaya. Work jaisa problem tha. Solve ho gaya.

Ek mover chair ko side se utha raha tha. Aarav ne kaha arms se mat, base se. Aadmi ne uski taraf dekha, phir grip badli. Aarav ne khud seat cover ki. Meera is chair mein saalon baithi thi. Armrest ka fabric ek jagah smooth ho gaya tha. Usne wrapping tape fabric par directly nahi lagne di.

Phone par message aaya.

> Keys mil gayi. Cleaning almost done. Lift available.

Usne reply kiya:

> Loading ho rahi. 20 min.

Uske baad photo bheji. Twelve boxes visible, chair wrapped. Meera ne thumbs-up bheja. Aarav ne photo save karne ka socha. Kyun, usse nahi pata tha. Usne chat band ki.

Last shelf panel utha toh floor ke peeche ek pencil mili. Yellow, half sharpened. Meera har draft par pencil se notes karti, phir keyboard par transfer. Aarav ne pencil desk par rakhi. Ab desk bhi nahi tha; dining table thi. Usne wahaan rakhi.

Movers ne final walk-through maanga. Usne bathroom, bedroom aur balcony dekhi. Meera ka towel drying stand par tha. Usne socha wet towel ab bhejna nahi chahiye. Wash karke baad mein. Picture bhej dega. Essentials ka small cloth bag door ke hook par tha.

Usne bag uthaya. Isme medicines thi.

Woh turant Meera ko call karne laga.

***

Meera nayi kitchen mein agent ke saath meter reading dekh rahi thi jab Aarav ka phone aaya.

"Door ka small bag reh gaya," usne kaha. "Medicines wala."

Usne aankhein band ki. "Shit."

"Truck mein daal doon?"

"Woh laptop charger bhi... haan. Please front mein, driver ke paas. Box mein nahi."

"Haan."

Agent ne meter number repeat kiya. Meera ne use likha, phir Aarav ko thanks kaha. Phone rakhne ke baad use panic ki chhoti wave aayi. Agar charger miss hota, medicines miss hoti, kuch manage ho jaata. Lekin woh ek din pehle check kar chuki thi. Usne list banaayi thi. Phir bhi reh gaya.

"Madam, sign yahan," agent ne kaha.

Usne sign kiya. Agent ne remaining instructions bataye. Gas connection, building office, water timings. Meera ne phone par notes liye. Door lock thoda tight tha. Agent ne kaha do-teen baar use karo, smooth ho jayega. Usne test kiya. Smooth nahi tha. Usne request kiya oil abhi kara dein. Agent ne watchman ko bulaya. Five minutes lage. Lock better hua.

Agent chala gaya. Meera new flat mein akeli khadi thi.

Window khuli thi. Outside ek tree tha, species usse nahi pata. Kisi branch par plastic bag atki thi. Neighbour ke kitchen se tadke ki smell aa rahi thi. Floor clean tha, lekin corner mein paint ki white dust thi. Usne bag rakhne ke liye jagah dhoondhi, phir yaad aaya poora room available tha.

Phone baja. Movers gate par. Woh neeche gayi.

Unloading mein one box ki bottom tape loose hui. Meera ne dono haath neeche rakhe. Aadmi ne kaha madam hat jao, heavy hai. Usne kaha books hain. Usne kaha haan, pata hai. Box safely floor par aaya. Koi book nahi giri. Meera phir bhi breath hold kiye thi.

Chair andar aayi. Panel wall ke paas. Driver ne small bag diya. Usne zip kholkar medicines, charger, spare keys check ki. Aarav ne ek transparent pouch mein pencil bhi daal di thi.

Usne pencil haath mein li, phir bag mein rakh di.

Movers ne signature maangi. Usne item count kiya. Twelve boxes. Chair. Panels. Suitcases. Ek suitcase ka wheel stuck tha; pehle se tha ya ab hua, woh sure nahi thi. Usne note kiya, team ko accuse nahi kiya. Payment settled. Aadmi ne drinking water poocha. Meera ke paas bottle thi, glasses nahi. Usne sealed bottle di. Unhone thank you kaha aur chale gaye.

Phone par Aarav ka message tha: sab pahunch gaya?

Usne type kiya:

> Haan. Chair safe. Bag bhi. Thanks.

Uska reply aaya:

> Good. Towel yahan hai. Wash karke rakh doonga.

Meera ne phone neeche kiya. Towel bhi.

Usse hasna tha. Usse rona tha. Instead usne small scissors nikali aur kitchen box ki tape kaati.

Naina one-twenty par aayi. Lemon rice, chai patti aur two bananas. Vihaan school mein tha. Usne door par Meera ko hug kiya, phir flat dekhkar kaha, "Light achhi hai. Plug points kam hain."

Meera ne extension board dikhaya. Naina ne haan kaha. Usne nahi poocha kisne diya.

Unhone floor par baith kar rice khaya. Naina ne poocha chair kahan rakhni? Meera ne window dikhayi. Dono ne desk ki height ke liye ek old folding table measure ki jo tenant chhod gaya tha. Thoda uneven, lekin temporary workable. Meera ko hansi aa gayi.

"Kya?" Naina ne poocha.

"Kuch nahi. Table."

Naina ne usse dekha, phir folding table ka leg check kiya. "Isme pin loose hai. Screwdriver chahiye."

Meera ne tools ka envelope dhoondha. Shelf brackets ke saath packed thi. Usne labelled box khola. Screwdriver mil gaya. Pin ko adjust kiya. Table steady ho gayi.

Naina ne chai banayi. Paani boil hote waqt Meera ne bathroom mein jaakar face dhoya. Mirror par old sticker ka glue tha. Usne ungli se scrape kiya. Glue nahi nikla. Uski aankhon se paani aa gaya. Woh sink ke saamne khadi rahi. Tap band tha. Outside Naina bartan dhoondh rahi thi, cupboard khol kar band kar rahi thi.

"Cup kahan?" usne poocha.

Meera ne mouth khola. Awaaz nahi aayi. Usne throat clear kiya.

"Kitchen box. Red mark."

"Mil gaya."

Usne tissue se face dabaya. Naina door par nahi aayi. Usne iske liye use silently thanks kaha.

Two-thirty par friend ko jaana tha. Vihaan ko pick karna tha. Meera ne kaha go, enough help ho gayi. Naina ne ek baar poocha raat ko call? Usne kaha text. Baat karne ka mann hua toh call karegi.

Naina ne shoes pehne. "Tu akeli reh sakti hai," usne kaha, phir turant add kiya, "Aur kisi ko bula bhi sakti hai. Main dono ke liye bol rahi hoon."

Meera ne smile ki. "Pata hai."

Door band hua. New lock ne click kiya. Woh room mein wapas aayi. Ab unpacked cups, half-open boxes aur chair thi. Empty room ki pehli smell thodi badal gayi thi. Chai, lemon rice, cardboard.

Usne work chair window ke paas rakhi aur baith gayi. Screen off thi. Woh kuch minutes bina kaam kiye chair par baithi rahi.

***

Aarav ne lunch mein poha garam kiya. Microwave ka bowl wahi tha jisme woh usually leftovers deta. Usne fork nikali, phir spoon li. Poha thoda dry tha. Paani sprinkle karke dobara garam kiya. Theek hua.

Uske baad usne living room saaf kiya. Dustpan mein tape ke chhote pieces, ek rubber band aur thin paper strips aayi. Bookshelf ki jagah wall ka rectangle halka tha. Sofa ab bhi tha. TV, fridge, dining table. Ghar empty nahi tha. Phir bhi har room mein kuch sound extra thi.

Usne guest room ki sheet nikaalne ka socha. Phir nahi ki. Main bedroom mein wapas jaana ek practical action hona chahiye tha. Usse declaration lag raha tha. Woh apne bag ko doorway tak laaya, phir guest bed par rakh diya. Aaj wahin so sakta tha. Koi rule nahi tha.

Three baje Kabir ka call aaya. Usne poocha shift ho gaya? Aarav ne haan kaha. Kabir ne evening walk offer ki. Aarav ne mana kar diya, phir kaha kal? Kabir ne Saturday better bola. Unhone Saturday six decide kiya.

Call ke baad Aarav ne calendar mein daala. Kabir ne laugh emoji bheja. Woh hasa nahi, lekin appointment saved dekhkar thoda better laga.

Five baje uski Ma ne call ki. Usne bataya Meera safely pahunch gayi. Ma ne poocha tu kaisa hai. Aarav ne usual theek bolna shuru kiya, phir ruk gaya.

"Aaj achha nahi lag raha," usne kaha.

Udhar silence hua. Phir Ma boli, "Main phone rakhoon ya baat karoon?"

Usne sofa par baithkar kaha, "Thodi der baat karo."

Ma ne Friday ke Papa ke follow-up ke baare mein bataya. Neighbour ki daughter ka admission. Gas cylinder ka rate. Aarav sunta raha. Beech mein Ma ne poocha khaaya? Usne haan kaha, poha. Unhone dinner ke liye dal banane ko kaha. Usne kaha fridge mein hai. Unhone relief ki saans li. Aaj usse woh sawal chhota nahi laga.

Shaam ko usne Meera ko towel ki photo bhejne ke liye phone uthaya, phir dekha chat mein already bata chuka tha. Usne message nahi bheja. Bag safely pahunch gaya, usne confirm kiya tha. Ab uski har hour ka update uska right nahi tha.

Woh balcony mein gaya. Do chairs thi. Ek usne table ke paas shift ki, kyunki damp leaves us par gir rahi thi. Phir khud doosri par baitha. Neeche traffic usual tha. Delivery rider, school van, vegetable cart.

Raat ko chai banayi toh brass strainer nahi mili. Usne third shelf kholi. Turmeric ke peeche thi. Usne use nikaala, chai chhaani aur wash karke stove ke paas hook par latka di.

Phone par Meera ka message aaya.

> First chai ban gayi. Gas working. Kal ka kaam bhi manage ho jayega.

Usne type kiya, proud of you. Phir delete kiya. Aaj uska proud hona uske permission jaisa kyun sound kar raha tha? Usne likha:

> Achha. Naina ka rice enough tha?

Reply aaya:

> Teen din ka hai. Obviously.

Uske muh par smile aayi. Usne likha, obviously. Phir phone rakh diya.

Chai finish karke usne cup sink mein rakha. Room mein koi nahi tha. Woh guest room ki taraf gaya, phir wapas aaya aur cup dho diya. Cup rack par rakhte hue uski sleeve phir geeli ho gayi. Usne fold upar ki aur kitchen ka tap achhi tarah band kiya.
