# Chapter 9 — Night Seven: If We Met Again Today

Wednesday ko movers ne eleven-thirty ki jagah ten baje aane ka message kiya.

Meera ne screen ko dekha, phir call kiya. Keys eleven ke baad milni thi. Loading ten-thirty tak kar sakte the, lekin unloading ka wait hoga. Aadmi ne kaha waiting charge. Usne original booking ka screenshot bheja. Usne kaha team se check karke batata hoon.

Uske baad usne landlord ko phone kiya. Current tenant ne keys agent ke paas chhod di thi. Cleaning subah nine tak start ho jayegi. Eleven baje mil sakti thi. Earlier possible nahi. Meera ne thanks kaha. Aaj har person ke paas ek alag clock thi.

Aarav ne office jaane se pehle poocha last night ke liye kuch chahiye? Usne kuch seconds socha. Flowers ka possibility uske mann mein aayi toh use almost panic hui.

"Khana simple," usne kaha. "Fridge mein jo hai."

"Okay."

"Aur packing tape. Khatam ho rahi hai."

"Do rolls?"

"Ek enough."

Woh haan bolkar gaya. Meera ne door band kiya. Aaj uski copy edit pending thi, rental agreement print karna tha, clothes pack karne the. Last night ka weight in tasks ke beech baar-baar uske haath rok raha tha. Woh folded kurta uthati aur sochti aaj yahan aakhri baar pehen sakti hoon. Phir use khud par irritation hoti. Kurta Friday ko bhi pehen sakti thi. Flat se jaa rahi thi, duniya se nahi.

Noon tak usne do suitcases close kiye. Third bag essentials ka tha. Chargers, medicines, ek towel, laptop, pouch. Card usme thi. Office ID documents ke saath. Chai ke liye saucepan book box mein nahi, kitchen box ke top par. Packing list usne phone aur notebook dono mein rakhi.

Apni cupboard ka khaali section dekhkar woh expect kar rahi thi kuch feel hoga. Bas dust dikhi. Usne cloth laakar pochh diya.

Naina ne call kiya. Kal noon ke baad aa sakti thi, pehle school ka ek event. Meera ne kaha keys lene jaate waqt cab hogi, move ke baad milna better. Naina ne poocha lunch laaoon? Usne haan kaha. Choice poochi toh usne jo available ho bolne ke bajaye lemon rice maanga.

"Finally specific demand," Naina ne kaha.

"Container leak mat karna."

"Ab review bhi?"

Woh dono hase. Phir Naina ne poocha, "Aaj kaise ho?"

Meera ne ceiling ki taraf dekha. "Baar-baar lag raha hai suitcase khol doon. Koi random cheez reh gayi hogi."

"Kholna hai toh khol."

"Actual mein nahi kholna."

Naina chup rahi. Meera ne us silence ko fill nahi kiya. Thodi der baad usne kaha, "Kal lunch ke saath chai patti bhi laana. Small packet."

Naina ne kaha done.

Three baje library ka email aaya. Trial session ke liye draft selection Friday tak bhejna tha. Meera ne calendar dekha. Thursday move. Friday setup. Usne Monday request kiya. Farah ka reply almost turant aaya: Monday works.

Usne breath chhodi. Itna hi karna tha. Woh har baar late tak jagkar schedule fit kar sakti thi. Har baar karna required nahi tha.

Seven baje Shaila ki video call thi. Meera ne phone dining table par books ke against tikaya. Ma ka face screen mein bahut paas tha. Unhe camera adjust karna aata tha, lekin jab worried hoti toh bhool jaati.

"Packing dikhao," unhone kaha.

Meera ne room ghumaya. Boxes dekhkar Ma ne poocha itne books kyun. Usne kaha unme kuch kitchen bhi. Phir mother ne green curtains ka packet dikhaya, courier kal bhejengi. Meera ne address forward kiya.

Aarav bell par aaya toh call ke beech tha. Usne door khola. Uske haath mein tape aur doodh tha. Woh quietly kitchen gaya. Shaila ne poocha Aarav aaya? Meera ne haan kaha.

"Hello bol doon?" Ma ne poocha.

Meera ne Aarav ki taraf dekha. Usne bags rakhkar phone ke frame mein head kiya. "Namaste, Aunty."

"Kaise ho?"

"Theek. Aap?"

"Haan. Khayal rakhna."

Ma ki aankhon mein paani aa gaya. Meera ne apna face screen se thoda hataaya. Aarav ne kaha, "Aap bhi."

Usne koi assurance nahi diya ki sab theek kar dega. Meera iske liye grateful thi. Ma ne usse poocha kal lunch milega na. Usne kaha Naina aa rahi. Phir call end karne mein chaar extra minutes gaye, kyunki har goodbye ke baad ek aur practical baat yaad aati rahi.

Phone band karke Meera ne table par flat rakha. Aarav tape nikaal raha tha. Ek roll laaya tha. Uske saath small scissors thi.

"Yeh kyun?" Meera ne poocha.

"Tum wali yahan reh gayi toh. Essentials mein daal do."

Usne scissors haath mein li. Cheap orange handle. Use achanak aankhon mein paani aaya. Woh drawer mein extra scissors se move kar sakti thi. Is chhote purchase se itna affect hona use inconvenient laga.

"Thanks," usne kaha.

"Rice banayein?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Khichdi. Thodi. Kal bache na."

***

Aarav ne dal measure ki. Meera ne kaha thodi aur, warna watery hogi. Usne spoon se add kiya. Kitchen mein do people the aur counter par four things. Phir bhi woh ek doosre ko touch kiye bina kaam kar rahe the.

Usne papad nikala. Ek corner se old smell aayi. Best-before dekha. Teen mahine pehle.

"Papad expire hota hai?" usne poocha.

"Har cheez ko chance dene ki zaroorat nahi."

Woh hasa aur packet rakh diya. Meera ne usse dekhkar kaha, "Dustbin. Cupboard nahi."

"Haan."

Usne phenk diya. Fridge mein half carrot thi. Curd tha. Pickle ka jar tha, oil upar. Usne Meera se poocha achar le jaogi? Usne kaha half de do. Do containers nikle. Spoon clean tha ya nahi usne check kiya. Aaj woh suddenly competent husband banne ka performance nahi karna chahta tha. Phir bhi uske haath har chhote kaam ko deliberately kar rahe the.

Khichdi cook hui toh woh table par baithe. Repair ke baad second dinner. Light usual tube light thi. Balcony ke paas yellow lamp off tha; uska plug boxes ke peeche chhup gaya tha. Aarav ne usse on karne ka socha aur nahi kiya. Dinner ko restaurant ki tarah stage karna galat lag raha tha.

Meera ne pehla bite lekar kaha ginger sahi hai.

"Kal se measured," usne kaha.

Woh hansi. "Good improvement."

Kuch minutes woh sirf khate rahe. Aarav ne movers ke timing ka poocha. Eleven-thirty restored thi. Keys lene Meera eleven par niklegi, loading team ko Aarav guide karega. Photos ke liye phone charged. Lift covers society morning mein lagwaayegi. New flat mein Naina noon ke baad, exact time school par depend.

"Main truck ke saath aa jaoon?" usne poocha.

Meera ne spoon plate mein rakha. "Main soch rahi thi first unpacking khud karoon. Naina ke saath."

Usne head hilaaya. Usse chot lagi. Woh logically prepared tha. Emotionally nahi.

"Keys kaise le jaogi? Cab?"

"Haan."

"Theek."

Meera ne usse dekha. "Tumhara aana help hota. Par..."

"Pata hai."

Usne doosra bite liya. Food warm tha. Throat se utarna difficult.

"Mujhe laga first day..." Meera ne shuru kiya.

"Meera, samajh raha hoon. Hurt ho raha hai. Par samajh raha hoon."

Usne haan kaha. Is baar usne explain karna band kiya. Aarav uske liye grateful tha, thoda gussa bhi. Woh dono feelings chupchaap khane ke saath rahi.

Dinner ke baad woh books ke box seal karne laga. Photograph ab bhi shelf par thi. Usne phone se uska scan kiya. Glass ke reflection ki wajah se image clear nahi aayi, yaad aaya glass alag rakha tha. Reflecting overhead light print ke gloss par thi. Usne window ke paas phone liya. Meera ne kaha angle thoda badlo. Usne badla. Scan theek aayi.

"Original tum rakh lo," usne kaha.

Meera ne photograph uthayi. "Tumhe chahiye?"

"Mere paas file hai."

"Print kar lena."

"Haan."

Woh photograph ko flat envelope mein rakhne lagi. Aarav dekh raha tha. Yeh koi grand gesture nahi tha. Woh almost kehna chahta tha original yahin rehne do. Usne nahi kaha.

Usne frame ka broken stand dustbin mein daala. Glass alag wrap kiya. Frame fix ho sakta tha, par aaj nahi.

***

Meera ka suitcase bathroom ke bahar khula tha. Toothbrush subah pack karna tha. Face wash, charger, slippers. Har cheez ke saamne use kal ka morning dikh raha tha.

Woh living room mein aayi toh Aarav balcony mein khada tha. Chair par kapde nahi the. Usne ek chair khud ke liye, doosri uske liye andar se kheench di. Usne poocha nahi baithogi. Meera baith gayi.

Neeche koi birthday cake laaya tha. Security cabin ke paas delivery boy aur resident naam confirm kar rahe the. Light blue box par baarish ki boondein thi. Society ke kisi ghar mein celebration tha. Usse woh unfair nahi laga. Bas strange laga ki itni alag evenings ek hi building mein fit hoti hain.

Aarav ne poocha, "Tumhare wahan balcony hai?"

"Chhoti. Ek chair. Plant ke liye corner."

"Ek hi?"

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. Usne add kiya, "Space. Matlab."

"Do fit ho sakti hain. Comfortable ek."

"Okay."

Woh dono kuch der road dekhte rahe. Meera ne apni knee par ungli se ek loose thread roll kiya. Uske andar ek question teen din se tha. Woh poochna nahi chahti thi, kyunki jawab se kal badal sakta tha. Phir use laga kal pehle hi badal gaya tha. Unhone bas truck book kiya tha.

"Agar main boloon abhi shift nahi karti," usne dheere kaha, "tum kya kahoge?"

Aarav ne turant uski taraf dekha. Meera ne add kiya, "Main bol nahi rahi. Pooch rahi hoon."

Usne nazar road ki taraf ki. Kaafi der jawab nahi aaya.

"Pehle toh haan," usne kaha. "Phir darr lagega ki kal se sab same."

Meera ne thread ko chair ke arm par rakha. "Mujhe bhi."

"Tumhe yahan rehna hai?"

Uske question mein hope thi. Meera use sun sakti thi. Use us hope ko na exploit karna tha, na jaldi se crush.

"Part of me," usne kaha. "Yeh ghar. Tum. Aadat. Sab. Pata nahi kitna kis cheez ka."

Aarav ne haath ek saath kiye. "Main tumhe rokna chahta hoon. Par mujhe nahi pata kiske liye rok raha. Tumhare liye, ya mujhe room khaali nahi dekhna."

Meera ne usse dekha. Woh ro nahi raha tha. Face par strain tha. Usse uska haath pakadne ka mann hua. Usne apne haath knee par hi rakhe.

"Hum try kar sakte hain," usne kaha. "Technically. Counselling. Dates. Jo nahi kiya."

"Haan."

"Par agar main yahan rukoon sirf is week ki wajah se..."

Woh ruk gayi. Uske paas next sentence perfect nahi tha. Aarav uska wait karta raha.

"Mujhe alag rehna hai," usne aakhir kaha. "Abhi. Yeh kehne mein itna guilt kyun aa raha hai?"

Aarav ne aankhein neeche ki. "Kyunki mujhe bura lag raha."

Uski simple honesty se Meera ki aankhon mein paani aa gaya. "Haan."

"Par tum ruk jaogi toh... mera bura lagna theek ho jayega? Bas uske liye toh nahi ruk sakti."

Usne sentence ke baad apna face rub kiya, jaise usse bhi woh bahut saaf lag gaya ho. "Bolna easy hai," usne add kiya. "Main kal shayad phir..."

"Pata hai. Main bhi."

Woh chup ho gaye. Meera ne balcony ki railing par jamahua paani dekha. Aarav ne Sunday ko drain saaf kiya tha; paani properly nikal raha tha. Woh bas us edge par thin droplets thi. Usne ek ungli se ek drop hata di. Doosri uski jagah aayi.

"Aaj hum milte toh?" Aarav ne kaha, thodi der baad.

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. "Kya?"

"Library mein. Tum abhi wali. Main abhi wala. Number dete?"

Usne aankhon ka paani tissue se pochha. "Pehle book wapas dete. Phir dekhte."

Woh hasa. "Fair."

"Tum abhi bhi first chapter padhkar review dete?"

"Ab summary bhi padh leta."

Meera ki hansi toot kar aayi. Usne hand apne face par rakha. Hansi aur rona alag nahi ho rahe the. Aarav ne tissue packet uski chair ke arm par rakha. Is baar usne uska haath pakad liya. Fingers thandi thi. Grip familiar. Usne is touch ko promise nahi banaya.

Kuch minutes baad usne haath chhoda.

"Kal jaa rahi hoon," usne kaha.

Aarav ne sir hilaaya. "Haan."

Room mein lautkar unhone mugs dho diye. Chai pi hi nahi thi; mugs din bhar ke the. Aarav ne poocha saucepan pack kar doon? Meera ne kaha subah ek baar use hoga. Usne stove ke paas rehne diya. Toothbrush bathroom mein rahe. Keys table par. Wallet bag mein. Move apni details ke saath wapas aa gaya.

Meera ne guest room ke door par ruk kar kaha, "Aaj tumse baat karke achha laga."

"Mujhe bhi."

"Iska matlab..."

Aarav ne dheere head shake kiya. "Pata hai."

Woh apne bedroom mein gayi. Bed bada lag raha tha. Pichhle chhe nights bhi isi bed par akeli soyi thi. Aaj usne side table ki drawer khol kar check ki. Empty. Hair clip li, lamp off ki. Phone charge par lagaya.

Thodi der baad knock hua. Aarav doorway par tha. Uske haath mein extension board.

"Naye flat mein desk ka plug door tha na. Yeh extra hai."

Meera ne board liya. "Tumhe?"

"Ek aur hai. TV ke paas."

Usne haan kaha. Aarav chala gaya. Woh board ko bag ke upar rakhkar bed par baith gayi. Ek second ko usse phir uthkar kehna tha mat jao, yahin baitho. Kuch bhi baat karte hain. Raat thodi aur stretch ho jayegi.

Usne door khula rehne diya.

Wall ke us paar Aarav ka fan speed two par chal raha tha. Click nahi thi. Woh let gayi. Kisi waqt aansu ruk gaye. Neend uske sochne ke kaafi baad aayi.
