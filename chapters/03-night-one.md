# Chapter 3 — Night One: When Did We Stop Being Happy?

Frame ka back kholne ke liye Aarav kitchen se butter knife laaya.

"Scissors yahin hai," Meera ne kaha.

"Point toot jayega."

"Butter knife ki permission li thi?"

Woh uski taraf dekha. "Kisse?"

"Kitchen department."

Usne knife ko halka sa salute karaya aur metal clips seedhi karne laga. Ek clip already missing thi. Doosri itni stiff ki ungli par nishaan pad gaya. Meera ne newspaper ki sheet par photograph rakhne ko kaha. Frame khul gaya toh peeche ek dead silverfish thi.

"Tumhare books ka tenant," Aarav ne kaha.

"Security deposit liya hoga tumne."

Usne tissue se use uthaya. Meera ne photograph sleeve se nikaali. Print ki edges par safed patli line thi. Upar kone mein orange colour thoda udd gaya tha. Naina ne date peeche likhi thi: 13/9/15. Last digit ka curve bilkul uske aaj ke handwritten corrections jaisa tha.

"Main kitna chhota lag raha hoon," Aarav ne kaha.

"The bhi."

"Twenty-four koi chhota nahi hota."

"Ab bata rahe ho. Tab toh bahut mature the."

"Main tha mature."

"Tumne mujhe bola tha volunteer hona easy hai, bas books uthani hain."

"Books hi uthayi thi."

"Do ghante baad bhaag rahe the."

"Bhaaga nahi tha. Lunch dhoondhne gaya tha."

Meera photo ko thoda roshni ki taraf le gayi. Usne apne chhote baal dekhe. Usse yaad aaya ki woh haircut khud ko pasand nahi tha. Naina ne kaha tha face khul gaya. Use lagta tha ears bahut dikh rahi hain.

Photograph mein woh ears ke baare mein nahi soch rahi thi.

***

September 2015 ke us Sunday, library ka side gate nahi khul raha tha.

Meera ek carton par baithe watchman ko samjha rahi thi ki van front gate se nahi aa sakti. Wahaan pandal ka bamboo laga tha. Watchman ne do baar kaha caretaker aayega. Caretaker ka phone switched off tha.

"Key toh hogi na," Meera ne kaha.

"Mere paas yeh hai."

Usne ek key dikhayi. Lock mein woh already teen baar try ho chuki thi.

Meera ne apne kurte ka dupatta tight kiya. Book sale ka poster usne proofread kiya tha. Boxes kis room mein jayenge, woh bhi usne list kiya tha. Side gate khulega ya nahi, usne nahi poocha tha. Ab har minute ek nayi van ya volunteer aata aur usi se poochta kahan jaana hai.

"Aap organisers mein ho?"

Peeche ek aadmi khada tha, grey T-shirt mein. Uske badge par volunteer likha tha, naam blank.

"Haan. Please boxes abhi mat utarwana."

"Utare nahi. Driver ko bol diya tea le lo."

"Achha."

"Yeh gate atka hai ya lock?"

"Lock."

Usne watchman se key maangi. Meera ne kaha, "Try ho gaya."

"Okay."

Aadmi ne key wapas kar di. Bas kar di. Usne unse zyada zor se try karke prove nahi kiya ki gate uske aane ka intezaar kar raha tha.

"Doosra route hai?" usne poocha.

Meera ne building ki taraf ishara kiya. "Andar se back corridor. Par stairs hain."

"Kitni?"

"Chaar."

"Chaar floor?"

"Chaar steps."

Uske face ka relief itna visible tha ki Meera has padi.

"Matlab workout se pehle information complete rakho," usne kaha.

Usne apna blank badge dekha. "Naam likh doon toh extra kaam milta hai?"

"Haan."

"Phir rehne deta hoon."

Woh van ke paas chala gaya. Meera ne poocha, "Naam kya hai waise?"

"Aarav. Kabir ke saath aaya hoon. Woh parking mein hai."

Usne black marker badhaya. "Ab kaam toh milega hi."

Aarav ne badge par naam likha. Last v ke neeche line zyada kheench gayi. Marker uske thumb par laga.

Phir teen ghante unhe baat karne ka proper waqt nahi mila.

Library ki old reference room mein sale thi. Donations ke books alag-alag condition mein the. Kuch par cover price ab bhi chipka tha, kuch par ghar ke phone numbers likhe the, ek cookbook ke beech atta tha. Meera ko staff ke liye curated selection lagana tha. Lekin unke publisher ke new titles wale boxes bheegne lage, toh woh floor par baith kar pages dry cloth se pochhne lagi.

Aarav heavy cartons utha raha tha. Beech mein usne kaha, "Aapko kaise pata kaunsa book kitne ka?"

"List hai."

"Aap list dekhti nahi."

"Kuch yaad hai."

"Mujhe apna ATM PIN do baar—"

"PIN mat batana," Meera ne turant kaha.

"Nahi bata raha. Bas yaad nahi rehta."

Woh hasta hua dictionary box ki taraf gaya. Usne ek book uthayi. "Iska buyer bhi aayega? Itna bada dictionary?"

"Aap prices laga do. Buyer main dekh loongi."

Do minute baad usne teen chhote dictionaries ko ek saath band kar diya. Meera ne kaha set nahi hai. Aarav ne tape khola. Phir unhone labels ke colours change kiye. Chhoti galti thi. Usne defend nahi kiya. Meera ko woh baat yaad rahi.

Sale shuru hui toh usse samajh aaya ki log books ke liye bhi bargain karte hain jaise sabzi ke liye. Ek retired teacher ne teen dictionaries liye. Aarav ne door se Meera ko teen ungliyan dikhayi. Meera ne eyebrows upar kiye, jaise kaha ho: bola tha.

Lunch tak uske pair dukh rahe the. Usne subah sirf toast khaya tha. Naina ko ek author ka call aa gaya; woh phone lekar corridor mein chali gayi. Meera cash box par baith gayi, khud ko samjhate hue ki crowd kam hote hi kuch khayegi.

Ek paper plate uske saamne aayi.

"Last do," Aarav ne kaha.

"Tumhara?"

"Ek mera. Ek aapka."

"Main abhi—"

"Cash main dekh leta hoon. Calculation books se easy hai. Hopefully."

Usne samosa liya. Baarish ki wajah se cover soft ho gaya tha. Chutney paper ke fold se bahar aa rahi thi. Aarav ne napkin dhoondhi, nahi mili, toh ek clean price-list ka back use de diya.

"Is par chutney laga di toh account mismatch hoga?" Meera ne poocha.

"Already zero rupees ka dictionary bech diya. Ab kya hi."

Usne use dekha.

"Joke," Aarav ne kaha.

"Please aise joke mat karo."

Woh sach mein embarrassed ho gaya. Meera hansi. Usne samosa ka half plate par rakh diya. "Tumhara kahan hai?"

"Kha liya."

Meera ne seedha uske haath dekhe. Chutney ka nishaan nahi tha. Na plate.

"Half lo."

"Main aur le loonga."

"Last do bola tha tumne."

"Aap bahut details—"

"Editor hoon."

Usne half le liya. Naina isi waqt aayi aur phone uthaya. "Evidence. Volunteers eating stock."

"Samosa stock ka part nahi," Meera ne kaha.

Photo click ho gayi. Aarav abhi bhi uski taraf dekh kar has raha tha. Photo mein woh dono camera ki taraf nahi dekh rahe the.

Sale ke end par gate finally khul gaya. Key caretaker ke bag mein thi. Usne maafi maangi aur kaha baarish mein scooter band ho gayi thi. Sab thake hue the; kisi ne properly gussa bhi nahi kiya.

Meera ke bag mein damp receipts thi. Bus stop tak pahunchne se pehle Aarav ne poocha, "Aapka naam?"

"Badge par hai."

Usne dekha. Badge dupatte ke neeche ulta hua tha.

"Meera," usne kaha.

"Achha. Main din bhar organise ji keh raha tha mann mein."

"Please woh naam mat rakhna."

"Ab toh mushkil hai."

Usne number nahi maanga. Meera ko thoda bura laga, phir woh khud par hansi. Teen ghante pehle toh naam bhi nahi pata tha.

Do din baad volunteer group mein Aarav ka message aaya. Kisi ne blue pen chhoda hai?

Meera ka blue pen lost nahi tha. Usne phir bhi message khola. Group mein photo thi. Pen ke saath ek book ke page par purple ink se likha tha: M. Sane.

Usne private reply kiya: pen nahi, book meri hai.

Aarav ka jawab tha: pen bonus hai. Return kaise karna hai?

Woh message usne teen baar padha tha. Return kaise karna hai. Jaise jawab mein date dena uski apni idea ho.

***

"Samosa tumne mera liya tha," Aarav ne kaha.

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. "Maine tumhe diya tha."

"Mere liye laaya tha Kabir. Main tumhe de raha tha."

"Toh half wapas mil gaya."

"Haan. Par full khane ka plan tha."

Woh hansi. Photograph ke neeche Naina ki handwriting ko thumb se cover kiya, phir hata diya.

"Yeh print usne kab diya tha?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Shaadi ke baad. Album bana rahi thi."

"Frame maine laaya tha."

"Haan, sale mein."

"Sale bolna zaroori hai?"

"Tumne sticker chhoda tha. Ninety-nine."

Usne frame ki back dekhi. Sticker ab bhi aadha tha. Price ka sirf last nine bacha tha.

Nine-thirty ho chuke the. Aarav ne chai poochi. Meera ne kaha agar tum bana rahe ho toh. Woh kitchen gaya, usne photograph newspaper par seedhi rakh di.

Shelf se books nikaalte hue use us first date ka route yaad aaya. Book return karne ke liye woh cafe gaye the. Aarav ne building ke wrong side park kiya tha. Woh dono baarish se bachne ko dukaanon ke shade ke neeche chal kar aaye. Table par usne poocha tha editor actual mein karta kya hai. Meera ne author aur comma ka ek boring example diya. Aarav ne follow-up poocha. Us waqt usse laga tha, kitna rare hai koi doosra sawal poochta hai.

Usne yeh baat use kabhi batayi thi? Shayad. Shaadi ke aas-paas log poochte the kis cheez se pyaar hua. Woh joke kar deti thi, usne samosa diya tha. Aarav kehta tha maine investment kiya.

Kitchen se gas lighter do baar click hua.

"Chini?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Half."

"Ab half?"

"Raat hai."

"Okay."

Meera ne photo ko us box mein nahi rakha. Frame se nikli cardboard ka back dustbin mein daalne lagi toh usne dekha, us par kabhi kisi ne pencil se ek measurement likha tha. Aarav har cheez hang karne se pehle measure karta tha. Phir bhi picture crooked lagti thi. Woh dono aankhon se adjust karte rehte, aakhir neighbour ne dekhkar kaha tha floor hi uneven hoga.

Balcony mein chai lekar woh dono baithe toh doosri chair par kapde the. Aarav ne pehle ek kurta uthaya, phir dupatta, phir ek damp towel. Meera ne kaha bedroom mein mat rakhna, smell aayegi. Woh drying stand lene andar chala gaya.

Do minute unke beech kuch bhi cinematic nahi tha. Stand ka ek leg atka. Window grill se chair tak distance kam tha. Usne knee se plant ko touch kiya; mitti floor par giri. Meera ne apni chair aage kheench li.

Finally woh baithe.

Road par ek delivery rider raincoat fold kar raha tha. Society ke saamne gutter ke paas paani bhara tha. Har car ke guzarte hi usse thoda peeche hona padta.

"Tab kitna time hota tha," Aarav ne kaha.

Meera ne cup pakad kar rakha. "Time tab bhi kam hota tha. Tum Saturday ko bhi kaam karte the."

"Par itna nahi."

"Haan."

"Aur tumhara office close tha."

"Mere ghar se. Tum mujhse milne forty minutes drive karte the."

Woh chai dekhne laga. Meera ko laga usne point bana diya. Usse turant regret hua. Har memory ko evidence banana zaroori nahi tha.

"Traffic bhi kam tha," usne add kiya.

Aarav ne halka sa haan kaha. Koi save nahi hua.

Baarish phir shuru hui. Boondein railing par pad rahi thi, un tak nahi aa rahi thi. Meera ne paaon chair ke neeche kiye.

"Last year Lonavala achha tha," Aarav ne kaha.

"Hmm."

"Tumhe achha nahi laga tha?"

Usne aankhein cup se uthayi. "Laga tha."

"Aise bola."

"Bas yaad kar rahi thi."

Us trip mein ek shaam woh market gaye the. Aarav ne uske liye embroidered pouch khareeda tha. Usne pouch ko almost roz use kiya tha. Agli subah breakfast ke waqt office call aa gaya. Twenty minutes ka bola, do ghante gaya. Meera ne room mein bag pack kiya, checkout call attend kiya, aur usse text kiya ki taxi neeche hai. Woh car mein ladte hue aaye the. Shaam ko pouch se lip balm nikaalte waqt usse laga tha gift accept karna dishonest hai.

Phir usne use kiya tha.

"Market wali shaam achhi thi," usne kaha.

"Haan."

"Checkout wali nahi."

Aarav cup ki rim par finger chala raha tha. "I know."

Meera ko usse apology nahi chahiye thi abhi. Shayad chahiye thi. Usse apne hi answers unclear lag rahe the.

"Maine kabhi nahi kaha ki har din kharab tha," usne kaha.

"Mujhe kabhi-kabhi aisa lagta tha."

"Kyunki main achhe din list nahi karti thi?"

"Kyunki jab bhi baat hoti thi, jo nahi hua woh hota tha."

Usne cup apne knee par rakha. "Jo hua woh tumhare saath jeeti thi na. Uske liye meeting thodi bulati."

Aarav ne dekha. Is baar uska face defensive nahi laga. Bas tired.

Meera ne dheere add kiya, "Shayad bolna chahiye tha. Kabhi."

Woh turant agree nahi hua. Usne photo ki taraf living room mein dekha.

"Mujhe woh first rental yaad aa raha tha," usne kaha. "Jab kitchen ka tap toot gaya tha. Bucket se bartan dhoye the. Tumne bola tha camping."

Meera muskurayi. "Three days."

"Do."

"Three. Plumber Wednesday ko aaya tha."

"Theek hai, three."

"Aur tum bucket bhar ke laaye the. Fourth floor. Lift nahi."

"Good exercise."

"Tab tum mazaak karte the. Ab plumber late ho toh mere saath bhi aise baat karte ho jaise main plumber hoon."

Woh sentence uski intention se zyada sharp nikla. Aarav ne cup door rakh diya.

"Main har baar tum par nahi chidh raha hota."

"Mujhe kaise pata chale?"

Usne jawab shuru kiya, phir ruk gaya. Neeche rider ne helmet pehen liya tha. Uski bike ek baar start nahi hui. Doosri baar ho gayi.

"Main ghar aake thak jaata tha," Aarav ne kaha.

"Main bhi."

"Pata hai."

Meera ko nahi laga usse pata tha. Lekin aaj uske paas usse prove karne ki energy nahi thi.

"Aaj nahi," usne kaha.

Woh seedha hua. "Kya?"

"Yeh. Kaun kitna thakta tha. Aaj mat."

"Main compare nahi—"

Usne khud ko roka. Chai ka last sip liya. "Okay."

Meera ko thodi der us silence mein uneasy laga. Woh khud conversation band karti thi, phir use bura lagta tha jab conversation band ho jaati. Naina ne kabhi casually kaha tha, tum usse bolne ka proper chance deti ho? Meera ne us waqt teen examples de diye the jahan diya tha. Baad mein socha tha, sawal ka jawab examples se diya jaa sakta hai, par kya woh poora jawab hai.

"Tumne woh book padhi thi?" usne poocha.

"Kaunsi?"

"Jo return karne aaye the. First time."

"Nahi."

Usne Aarav ko dekha.

"Half," usne kaha.

"Half bhi?"

"First chapter."

Meera ki hansi bina warning nikli. "Tumne bola tha interesting hai."

"First chapter interesting tha."

"Aur main forty minutes uske ending par—"

"Isliye nahi padhi phir. Tumne ending bata di."

Woh kuch seconds hasti rahi. Aarav bhi hasa. Phir usne kaha, "Us din tumhe sunna achha laga tha. Book se zyada."

Meera ne cup par nazar rakh li. Sentence sundar tha. Use us par bharosa karne ke liye thoda waqt chahiye tha. Kyunki aaj bhi kabhi-kabhi woh use sunna chahti thi, aur phone par ek chhoti vibration usse beech mein chhod deti thi.

"Mujhe bhi," usne kaha. "Tumse baat karna."

Aarav ne sir hila diya. Woh unke beech kisi promise ki tarah nahi rakha gaya. Baarish ki smell thodi tez ho gayi thi. Meera ko thand lagi.

Andar aakar usne cups sink mein rakhe. Aarav ne frame ka glass saaf kiya. Photograph dobara frame mein rakhne ko usne haath badhaya, phir poocha, "Yeh tum le jaogi?"

"Abhi decide nahi kiya."

"Scan kar sakte hain."

"Haan. Par abhi rehne do."

Usne frame ke bina photo bookshelf par tika di. Neeche ek dictionary thi, jise Aarav pehle box mein daal raha tha. Usne dictionary wahi rehne di, taaki photo seedhi tik sake.

"Goodnight," usne kaha.

"Goodnight."

Bedroom mein usne drawer se earplugs nikaale, phir rakh diye. Guest room ka fan click karta tha. Pehle bhi karta tha, jab guests rehte the. Aarav ne kitni baar kaha tha replace karna padega. Dono ne tab tak postpone kiya tha jab tak koi aur raat bhar us awaaz ke neeche na soye.

Meera bed par baith kar apna clip kholne lagi.

Wall ke us paar se chair kheenchne ki awaaz aayi. Phir fan band hua. Kuch seconds baad phir chala.

Usne phone uthaya. Type kiya: speed 2 par click kam hai.

Send karke use laga bolne bhi jaa sakti thi.

Udhar fan ki speed badli.

Phone par reply aaya.

> Haan. Thanks.

Meera ne phone ulta rakh diya. Usse neend aane mein ab bhi kaafi der thi.
