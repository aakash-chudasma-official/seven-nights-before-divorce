# Chapter 7 — Night Five: I Was Right, But I Lost You

Library mein Monday ko coordinator nahi thi.

Meera ne phone karne se pehle apna introduction teen baar socha tha. Call utha toh kisi aur ne kaha madam field visit par hain, kal afternoon try karo. Meera ne poocha koi email address? Aadmi ne spelling batayi. Usne repeat ki. Connection toot gaya. Usne dobara phone nahi kiya; website par address mil gaya.

Email mein usne reading sessions ka short proposal bheja. Abhi shop ki baat nahi. Chaar Sundays, existing room, limited seats. Voluntary karna zaroori tha ya honorarium possible, woh bhi poocha. Last line likhte waqt use awkward laga. Phir usne send kiya.

Uske baad laundry dry ki, proof par do ghante kaam kiya aur nayi society ke office mein tenant form bheja. Aadmi ne spouse details ka blank wapas highlight kar diya.

Meera ne screen dekhi. Married likhna factual tha. Aarav ka number emergency contact banana ab sahi tha ya nahi, woh decide karna baaki tha.

Usne Naina ko message kiya: emergency contact de sakti hoon tumhara?

Reply aaya: haan, but mere phone ka silent mode lethal hai. Ma ka bhi do.

Usne dono numbers diye. Spouse details alag field mein bhari. Ek form mein uski present life ko fit karne ke liye teen explanations lag rahi thi.

Aarav ghar aaya toh table par carpenter ka quotation tha.

"Yeh kya hai?" usne bag rakhte hue poocha.

"Bracket ka. Mrs D'Souza ne number diya."

"Kisko dikhaya?"

"Photo bheji. Woh aayega toh actual dekhega."

"Tumne book kiya?"

"Kal six baje."

Aarav ne paper uthaya. "Tumhe jaana hai, toh mere table ka time bhi decide kar diya?"

Meera ne usse dekha. Woh abhi doorway se do steps hi andar aaya tha.

"Tumhara table?"

Usne paper neeche kiya. "Mera matlab yahan rehne wala."

"Haan. Book kar diya. Cancel kar sakte hain."

"Pehle pooch sakti thi."

"Chhe saal se pooch rahi hoon."

Six years exaggeration thi. Table unhone 2019 mein liya tha; wobble current flat mein move ke baad shuru hui thi. Meera ko yeh timeline yaad thi. Usne phir bhi sentence correct nahi kiya.

"Bracket loose hai," Aarav ne kaha. "Main kar sakta hoon."

"Toh kar do."

"Abhi aaya hoon."

"Aaj nahi kaha."

Usne shoes utaare. Ek shoe rack ke bahar reh gaya. Meera ki nazar us par gayi, phir usne apna laptop khola. Agar usne shoe bhi kaha toh woh point prove kar dega jiska use darr tha.

"Kitna charge?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Minimum visit five hundred. Material extra."

"Five hundred ek screw ke liye?"

"Phir cancel kar do."

"Main discuss kar raha hoon."

"Tumne shuru hi aise kiya jaise maine tumhare account se loan le liya."

Usne shirt ke sleeves fold kiye. "Yeh baat paise ki nahi."

"Abhi five hundred tumne poocha."

"Because mujhe pata hona chahiye na mere ghar mein kya ho raha."

Meera ne laptop band kiya.

"Ab tumhara ghar bhi ho gaya. Theek hai. Main Thursday ko—"

"Oh, come on."

Woh awaaz unchi nahi thi. Dismissal kaafi tha. Meera ko chest mein us familiar heat ka ehsaas hua. Usse jitna gussa table par hona chahiye tha, usse zyada hone laga.

"Main yeh faltu kaam kyun kar rahi hoon?" usne kaha. "Mujhe jaana hai. Tumhara bracket, tumhara table, tumhara ghar."

Aarav ek moment chup raha. Phir bola, "Maine ek word bola aur tumne poori kahaani bana di."

"Do baar bola."

"Great. Count bhi hai."

Meera khadi hui. Usne quotation uthaya, phone se carpenter ka chat khola aur type karne lagi.

"Kya kar rahi ho?"

"Cancel."

"Meera, phone rakho."

"Tumne poochne ko bola. Pooch liya. Nahi chahiye."

Woh uske haath ki taraf badha. Chhua nahi. Meera turant peeche hui. Dono ruk gaye. Aarav ne haath neeche kar liya.

"Main phone nahi le raha," usne kaha.

"Toh mujhe bolo mat kya karna."

Usne message send kar diya: sorry, kal ka visit hold kar dijiye. Confirm karke bataoongi.

Uske baad room ki awaaz bahut clear ho gayi. Fridge ka motor. Neeche scooter ka horn. Aarav ki saans.

Woh guest room ki taraf gaya. Meera ke muh se nikal gaya, "Haan. Jao."

Usne door ke paas ruk kar kaha, "Mujhe half an hour chahiye."

"Phir?"

"Phir baat karoonga."

"Har baar—"

"Half an hour," usne repeat kiya. "Door slam nahi kar raha. Main bhaag nahi raha. Bas abhi jo bolunga aur kharab hoga."

Meera ne jawab nahi diya. Woh andar gaya. Door almost band ki, poori nahi.

Usse timer lagana absurd laga. Phir bhi usne clock dekhi. Seven-twelve.

***

Aarav ne shirt badli, phir usi shirt ko haath mein pakde khada raha.

Office mein aaj ek junior ne client ko galat attachment bheji thi. Usne usse calmly kaha tha undo nahi kar sakte, correct version aur short note bhej do. Ghar par ek carpenter ki booking se uska tone badal gaya tha.

Uske paas reasons the. Usse bina bataye time choose hua. Quotation unnecessary tha. Meera move ki tension mein har unfinished task ko urgent bana rahi thi. Woh reasons ek-ek karke dimaag mein aaye. Unke peeche aaj office mein client ne jo kaha tha woh bhi tha: I need someone senior on this.

Woh senior hi tha. Phir bhi call par uske boss ko add karna pada. Boss ne wahi explanation diya aur client satisfied ho gaya.

Usne yeh ghar aate hi batana nahi socha tha. Use laga tha thodi der chup rahega, phir normal. Table ka paper dekha aur use laga uski absence mein ek aur person decide kar chuka hai ki woh capable nahi.

Meera ne aisa kaha nahi tha.

Woh bed par baitha. Phone par seven-nineteen tha. Twenty-three minutes abhi baaki the. Use lag raha tha abhi jaakar clear kar de. Lekin clear ka matlab apne reasons explain karna hoga. Meera phir kahegi, tum explanation de rahe ho. Usko gussa aa jayega. Woh wahi line dohra dega: main discuss kar raha hoon.

Usne phone bed par rakha.

Table usne choose kiya tha. Meera ko lighter colour pasand tha. Shop mein dark wood discount par thi. Aarav ne kaha tha sturdier hai. Meera ne top par knuckles se tap karke poocha, sturdy ki sound kaise pehchaante ho? Usne kaha salesman ne bola. Woh dono hase the. Flat mein laane ke baad table unke first dinner mein hi wobble hui thi. Floor uneven tha ya bracket, tab clear nahi hua. Aarav ne cardboard fold kiya. Meera ne kaha temporary. Usne kaha obviously.

Current flat mein bracket dikh gaya tha. Spare screw dhoondhna tha. Usne online parts dekhe. Phir postpone hua. Baad mein Meera ke har mention mein use instruction sunai deta tha. Woh jaan-boojhkar nahi karta, aisa usne khud ko kaha. Bas right screwdriver chahiye tha, time chahiye tha.

Kal shelf kholne ke liye right screwdriver usi toolbox se nikla tha.

Usne head apne haathon mein rakha. Ek table fix karna mushkil nahi tha. Table ki baat par apni insult na sunna mushkil ho gaya tha.

Usse October ki woh evening yaad aayi, jab Meera ne shop ka spreadsheet khola tha. Uski ice cream melt ho rahi thi. Woh same screen par numbers dekh raha tha. Column mein rent tha, salaries optional thi, deposit blank tha. Usne formula correct kiya toh projected profit turant loss mein badal gaya.

Us waqt usse sach mein darr laga tha.

Savings koi limitless cushion nahi thi. Papa ka next test, rent renewal, car service. Meera ke last do invoices delayed the. Usne mann mein calculate kiya tha ki agar shop fail hui toh kitna time recover karne mein lagega. Us calculation mein Meera ka hurt face include nahi hua tha. Woh usse photograph ki tarah yaad nahi tha; bas laptop band hone ki awaaz yaad thi.

Woh sahi tha ki plan incomplete tha.

Phir usne next morning article bheja. Usme first-year failures ke statistics the. Aarav ko laga useful research hai. Meera ne reply nahi kiya toh usne assume kiya woh numbers se bhaag rahi hai. Usne ek baar bhi poocha nahi ki chhota version kya ho sakta hai. Usne khud bhi kuch alternatives soche the: home-based book club, library collaboration. Lekin unhe bola nahi. Us waqt use laga tha ab main hi kyun sab sochoon.

Aaj Saturday ki baat yaad karke usne bed ka edge pakda. Library contact pehle se tha. Woh khud usse nahi jaanta tha. Dono alag-alag apne argument ke side mein ideas bachate rahe.

Seven-forty-one.

Woh utha. Water bottle li, ek sip piya. Phir cupboard ka door bandh karne laga toh hinge atak gayi. Usne ek second force lagayi. Door ne loud click kiya. Living room mein chair ki awaaz ruk gayi.

"Cupboard," usne unchi awaaz mein kaha. "Door atki thi."

Koi reply nahi aaya.

***

Meera ne half an hour mein chaar mails padhe aur ek bhi answer nahi kiya.

Usne carpenter ka reply dekha. Aadmi ne sirf okay bheja tha. Uske liye yeh ek rescheduled visit thi. Na disrespect, na eight-year marriage ka evidence. Usse apna message embarrassing laga.

Seven-forty-four par Aarav aaya. Meera ne clock dekhte pakde jaane se bachne ke liye screen par nazar rakhi.

"Main bol sakta hoon?" usne poocha.

"Permission kyun—"

Woh ruki. Har opening ko wrong bana dene se woh andar kaise aayega?

"Haan," usne kaha.

Aarav opposite chair par baitha. Table hili. Dono ne is baar usse ignore kiya.

"Office mein aaj bura din tha," usne kaha.

Meera ka face apne aap tight hua. Usne dekha toh turant add kiya, "Excuse nahi. Bas... ghar aate hi laga tumhe bhi lagta hai mujhse nahi hoga."

"Carpenter isliye nahi bulaya."

"Pata hai. Ab."

"Tum kar sakte ho. Karte nahi."

Usne aankhein neeche ki. "Haan."

Meera ne chair se back hata li. Usse expected tha woh bolega time nahi. Woh jawab ready tha. Ab uske paas apna next sentence nahi tha.

"Mujhe pehle poochna tha," usne kaha. "Time ka."

"Haan."

"Par tum 'mere ghar' bolo toh..."

Aarav ne aankhein uthaayi. "Galat tha."

Meera ko phir bhi gussa tha. Usne kaha, "Aur tum keh rahe the kahaani bana di. Jaise main paagal hoon."

"Maine paagal nahi—"

Woh dono ruk gaye. Aarav ne apne fingers table se hata liye.

"Tumhe dismiss kiya," usne kaha. "Haan."

Meera ne phone ulta kiya. "Mujhe tumse har word admit nahi karwana."

"Toh kya chahiye?"

Question thoda sharp tha. Usne apne mouth par haath phiraya. "Sorry. Yeh bhi sahi nahi nikla."

Meera ki aankhon mein paani aa gaya. Use irritation hui. Carpenter. Table. Kitna stupid tha. Aur kitna stupid nahi tha.

"Mujhe nahi pata ab kya chahiye," usne kaha. "Pehle chahiye tha ki tum bolo haan, reh gaya, kara dete hain. Bas. Main manager na banoon, tum accused na bano. Ab... ab main jaa rahi hoon. Phir bhi hum yahi kar rahe."

Aarav kuch der usse dekhta raha. Phir kaha, "Main tumhe jaane ke liye punish nahi karna chahta."

Meera ne uski taraf dekha. Us sentence ka khayal uske mann mein aaya tha; woh bol nahi paayi thi.

"Mujhe aisa laga," usne kaha.

"Pata hai. Ya ab lag raha hai ki lag sakta hai."

Woh precise rehna chahta tha. Meera ko uski precision kabhi kind lagti thi, kabhi lawyer jaisi. Aaj dono.

"Maine bhi chhe saal bola," usne kaha. "Teen-chaar honge."

"Table ab bhi kharab hai."

Usne bahut halki hansi nikali. "Haan."

Woh chup baithe. Meera ke mail ka notification aaya. Usne dekha nahi. Aarav ne poocha kal ka slot wapas le sakte hain? Usne kaha poochna padega. Usne phone uthaya, phir uski taraf badhaya. "Tum kar lo. Number yahin hai."

Aarav ne apne phone mein number type kiya. Message bheja. Visit Monday nahi, Tuesday thi. Usne six confirm kiya aur calendar mein daala. Phir Meera ko phone diya nahi, kyunki usne uska liya hi nahi tha. Dono apne phones pakde baithe the. Meera ko is pointless detail par hansi aa gayi.

"Kya?" Aarav ne poocha.

"Kuch nahi."

Is baar woh insist nahi hua.

Unhone dinner ka decide kiya. Fridge mein khichdi nahi bachi thi; kal ki dal thi. Meera ne roti ka dough nikala. Aarav ne plates lagayi. Table phir hilli. Usne folded paper nikaal kar temporary wedge banaya, phir Meera ki taraf dekha.

"Kal tak," usne kaha.

"Haan."

Khana khate waqt reading pilot ka reply aaya. Coordinator ne Tuesday four baje call ka time diya tha. Meera ne Aarav ko bataya. Usne poocha tum available ho? Uska pehla impulse tha kehna, main manage kar loongi. Phir use samajh aaya woh bas schedule pooch raha tha.

"Haan. Four kar loongi."

Usne email mein confirm kiya.

Raat ko work chair se uthte hue Meera ne ek box ki edge se paaon takra diya. Gussa turant aaya. Usne box ko unnecessary zor se side kiya. Aarav ne dekha, par kuch nahi kaha. Usne khud box seedha kiya. Books andar thi; corners damage ho sakte the.

Bedroom ke door par pahunchkar usne kaha, "Half an hour ke baad aaye. Thanks."

Aarav phone se dekha. "Teen minute late."

"Do."

Woh muskuraya. Meera bhi. Phir door ke andar aate hi uski aankhon mein paani wapas aa gaya. Thodi si achhi conversation se uska poora gussa nahi gaya tha. Table ki booking ho gayi thi. Uski chest ka pressure abhi bhi tha.

Usne light off nahi ki. Bed par pair fold karke baithi rahi. Living room se Aarav ke shoes rack mein rakhne ki awaaz aayi. Ek ke baad doosra.

Usne phone par Tuesday ka reminder dekha. Uske neeche Thursday shift.

Dono dates wahi rahi.
