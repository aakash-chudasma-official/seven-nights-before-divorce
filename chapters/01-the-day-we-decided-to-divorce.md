# Chapter 1 — The Day We Decided to Divorce

Lift wale aadmi ko fridge ka size chahiye tha.

"Fridge nahi jaa raha," Aarav ne kaha.

Phone aur kaan ke beech uski shirt ka collar aa gaya tha. Usne gardan seedhi ki. Laptop par ek red cell blink kar raha tha. Warehouse se kisi ne phir wrong version bhej diya tha.

"Sir, list mein likha hai kitchen items."

"Haan. Bartan. Chhote appliances. Fridge nahi."

"Washing machine?"

Aarav ne dining table ke us paar dekha. Meera ek delivery envelope khol rahi thi. Scissors uske haath mein thi; envelope phir bhi nahi khul raha tha.

"Washing machine bhi nahi," usne kaha.

"Madam se confirm kar lo. Baad mein truck chhota pada toh—"

"Main yahin hoon," Meera ne kaha. "Speaker par karo."

Usne phone table par rakh diya.

Meera ne aadmi ko teen baar samjhaya ki ek hi flat se do alag gharon ka samaan nahi jaa raha. Sirf ek person shift ho raha tha. Ek work chair, do chhoti shelves, books, clothes, kitchen ke kuch boxes. Bed nahi. Sofa nahi. Dining table abhi decide nahi hua tha.

"Single person shifting," aadmi ne aakhir kaha.

"Haan."

"Toh pehle bolna tha na, madam."

Call katne ke baad phone kuch seconds tak table par chamakta raha.

Aarav ne laptop band nahi kiya. Screen ko bas thoda neeche jhuka diya, jaise us angle par kaam unhe dekh nahi sakta.

"Table bhi nahi jaa raha?" usne poocha.

"Mere wahan fit nahi hoga. Photo bheja tha tumhe."

"Dekha tha."

"Toh."

Usne haan mein sir hila diya. Pashan wale flat ki photo usne office mein kholi thi. Ek window, do plastic chairs, corner mein pocha. Meera ne neeche dimensions likhe the. Woh photo dekhte hue usne client ko likha tha: we'll resolve before end of day.

Ab usse yaad nahi tha window kis taraf thi.

Kitchen mein cooker ki seeti hui. Meera uthi, gas band ki aur wapas envelope ke paas aa gayi. Is baar usne scissors rakh kar glue wali side phaad di.

Andar broadband ka refund form tha.

"Yeh tumhare naam par hai," usne paper uski taraf sarakaya. "Connection rehne do. Main apna naya lagwa loongi."

"Transfer ho sakta hai."

"Tumhe bhi toh chahiye."

"Haan."

Aarav ne form utha liya, phir rakh diya. Har cheez ki do copies nahi hoti thi. Kuch cheezein khareedni padengi. Usse pata tha. Phir bhi din bhar woh aise suggestions de raha tha jaise thodi efficiency se second household ki zaroorat hi khatam ho jayegi.

Thursday tha. Baarish dopahar se ruk-ruk kar ho rahi thi. Balcony ki railing par Meera ka ek sock chipka tha. Neeche society ke security cabin ke paas school van ke bachche jhagad rahe the ki umbrella kaun pakdega.

"Owner ka message aaya?" usne poocha.

"Keys next Thursday, eleven ke baad. Same date."

"Cleaning?"

"Subah karwa dega. Jo tenant hai, woh Wednesday night tak nikal raha hai."

"Confirm kar lena."

Meera ne envelope ka phata tukda apni ungli se fold kiya.

"Kar liya."

"Bas keh raha tha."

"Maine bhi bas bataya."

Aarav ko jawab dene ke liye ek familiar sentence mil gaya tha: tum har baat ko aise kyun leti ho. Usne use nahi kaha. Koi bada decision nahi tha. Woh thak gaya tha.

Pichhle Sunday unhone isi table par decide kiya tha ki aaj families ko bata denge. Usse pehle teen mahine mein kitni baar baat shuru hui aur adhoori reh gayi, uska accurate count uske paas nahi tha. Meera ke paas shayad ho. Ek baar unhone counsellor ke numbers save kiye the. Ek introductory call hui thi; uske baad dono ne agle appointment ke liye dates bhejne ka promise kiya. Aarav ne ek week maanga. Meera ne follow-up nahi kiya. Do hafton baad usne kaha tha, "Hum appointment bhi tabhi lenge jab main chase karoon?"

Usne kaha tha, "Tum le leti."

Phir baat appointment ki nahi rahi thi.

June mein legal consultation hua tha. Us chhote office mein AC zyada thanda tha. Unhone practical questions pooche the. Documents ki list li thi. Kisi ne unhe yeh nahi kaha tha ki ghar jaakar ek paper sign kar do aur baat khatam. Aarav ko woh process ki complexity ab bhi ajeeb tarah se comforting lagti thi. Forms mein dates hoti thi. Feelings mein nahi.

"Ma ko pehle tum call karogi?" usne poocha.

Meera ki ungli ruk gayi.

"Haan."

"Main bahar chala jaoon?"

"Nahi. Matlab... room mein jaa sakte ho. Agar tumhe call aaye toh please aise mat bolna ki maine suddenly decide kiya."

"Main aisa kyun boloonga?"

"Pata nahi. Bas keh rahi hoon."

Usse bura laga. Phir yaad aaya, ek family dinner mein usne bina soche kaha tha ki Meera ko freelance karna tha, isliye usne job chhodi. Us waqt usse statement bilkul factual laga tha. Us raat car mein Meera ne poocha tha, "Humne jo baat ki thi, woh kahan gayi?"

"Main nahi boloonga," Aarav ne kaha.

Laptop par notification aaya. Usne screen ko aur neeche kar diya.

***

Meera ne Ma ka naam khol kar phone rakh diya.

Pehle cooker khola. Dal mein thoda paani kam tha. Paani daala, spoon chalaya, lid side mein rakhi. Phir gas ke neeche gira hua haldi ka daag pochha.

Ab aur kuch karne ko nahi tha.

Phone uthaya toh Naina ka message tha.

> Sofe ka offer abhi bhi valid hai. Bas Vihaan 6 baje cartoon shuru karega. No five-star promises.

Meera muskurayi. Naina ne Tuesday ko kaha tha ki woh bags lekar aa jaye. Books baad mein. Meera ne mana kiya tha. Do baar packing, do baar cab, editing files idhar-udhar. Naina ke ghar mein guest room nahi tha; living room mein bachche ki school project wali volcano padi thi. Usse wahan jaakar har cheez ke liye sorry nahi bolna tha.

Aur woh apna ghar chhodne ke liye ek date chahti thi, ek bhaagne ka moment nahi.

Usne reply kiya:

> Thanks. Thursday fix hai. Agar kuch change hua toh boloongi.

Usne rental budget notebook kholi. ₹15,500 rent. Deposit ka transfer ho chuka tha. Retainer payment time par aaya toh September theek tha. Agar nahi aaya toh bhi do mahine ka buffer tha, lekin woh buffer car repair ya family emergency ko include nahi karta tha. Naye mattress ke aage usne question mark lagaya. Filhaal foldable wala kaam karega.

Ma ko budget batana bekaar tha. Woh pehle poochengi ki Aarav kya keh raha hai.

Call doosri ring par uth gaya.

"Haan, beta. Main tumhe hi karne wali thi. Woh jo achar bheja tha, khola?"

"Haan."

"Oil upar rehne dena. Pichhli baar tumne nikaal diya tha, phir fungus—"

"Ma, thodi baat karni thi."

Udhar steel ka koi bartan rakha gaya.

"Kya hua?"

"Main next Thursday shift kar rahi hoon. Alag. Aundh se Pashan."

Ma ne kuch nahi kaha.

Meera ne pehle se socha hua sentence bola. "Aarav aur maine kaafi time se baat ki hai. Hum separation aage le jaa rahe hain."

"Kis baat par?"

"Ek baat nahi hai."

"Par abhi toh anniversary thi."

Meera ne fridge ki taraf dekha. Anniversary par liya cake ka plastic knife ab bhi magnet ke peeche phansa tha. Cake achha tha. Unhone aadha neighbour ko diya tha, kyunki dono ko dinner ki bhookh nahi thi.

"Haan."

"Tum log aaye bhi toh the July mein. Mujhe toh—"

"Humne bataya nahi tha."

"Aarav ghar par hai?"

"Haan."

"Phone do."

Meera ne aankhein band ki. "Pehle meri baat sun lo."

"Main sun hi rahi hoon. Par woh kya keh raha hai?"

Guest room ke darwaaze ke neeche se Aarav ke laptop ki halki roshni aa rahi thi. Usne darwaaza poora band nahi kiya tha.

"Woh bhi yahi keh raha hai. Yeh meri akeli announcement nahi hai."

"Tum dono thak gaye hoge. Aajkal kaam hi itna—"

"Ma."

Apni awaaz ki sakhti use pasand nahi aayi. Ma ki khamoshi mein usne apna reflection kitchen ki dark window mein dekha: hair clip aadhi khuli, T-shirt par dal ka chhota daag.

"Sorry. Main yeh keh rahi hoon ki hum pehle se soch rahe the. Aapko batana baaki tha."

Ma ne poocha, "Koi aur hai?"

Meera ne kursi kheench kar baithna behtar samjha.

"Nahi."

"Uski taraf se?"

"Nahi, Ma."

"Phir..."

Sentence poora nahi hua. Meera usse poora kar sakti thi. Phir itna bada decision kyun. Phir koshish kya ki. Phir aath saal ka kya.

"Main abhi sab explain nahi kar paoongi," usne kaha. "Bas aapse chhupana nahi tha."

Ma ne kaafi der baad poocha ki naya flat safe hai ya nahi. Society ka naam likha. Deposit kitna tha. Landlord family hai ya agent. Meera ne answers diye. Ek sawal par usse address dekhna pada. Ma ne turant kaha, "Tumne poori enquiry ki hai na?"

Woh kehna chahti thi ki haan, usne apni zindagi mein pehli baar koi flat nahi liya. Phir Ma ki saans sunai di, phone ke bilkul paas. Thodi uneven.

"Haan," Meera ne dheere kaha. "Naina ne bhi dekha hai."

Call ke end par Ma ne kaha, "Main Friday aa sakti hoon."

"Abhi mat aao. Mujhe pehle settle hone do."

"Main bas help—"

"Pata hai. Main boloongi."

Usne phone rakh diya. Aankhon mein paani nahi tha. Gale mein ek ajeeb sa pressure tha, jaise bahut tez seedhiyan chadh kar aayi ho.

Aarav door par khada tha.

"Paani?" usne poocha.

"Yahin hai."

Glass khaali tha. Usne dekha, phir khud uth kar bhar liya.

"Meri Ma ko bhi bata doon?" Aarav ne kaha.

"Haan. Aaj hi."

"Main bas—"

"Aaj. Please."

Woh sir hila kar balcony mein chala gaya.

Meera ne dal ka lid uthaya. Ab woh bilkul sahi consistency mein thi. Usse gussa aaya ki woh ab bhi yeh notice kar rahi thi.

***

Aarav ki Ma ne pehle kaha ki TV ki awaaz mein sunai nahi de raha. Phir Prakash ko bulaya. Phir poocha ki kya dono line par hain.

"Meera kitchen mein hai. Main bata raha hoon."

"Usse bulao."

"Ma, pehle meri suno."

Usne jo bataya, beech mein khud ko teen baar correct kiya. 'Thoda space' kaha toh Meera ka warning wala sentence yaad aaya. Space holiday jaisa lagta tha. Usne kaha, "Alag rehne ka decision liya hai. Divorce ke process ke liye advice li hai. Abhi kuch final legally nahi hua."

Papa chup the. Unke phone ka microphone kapde se rub ho raha tha.

"Kya hua hai?" Ma ne poocha.

Uske paas woh list thi jo woh apne mann mein arguments ke baad banata tha. Chhoti baat ko bada karna. Har discussion mein purani baat. Use kaam karte dekhkar irritate hona. Aaj list bolne mein kuch galat laga. Jaise woh absent person ki complaint register kar raha ho.

"Kaafi cheezein," usne kaha. "Dono se."

"Aisa toh har ghar—"

"Ma, pata hai."

Papa pehli baar bole. "Rent ka kya karoge?"

Aarav ko almost relief hua. Ek answer jo usse aata tha.

Usne lease batayi. November tak rahega. Phir chhota flat dekhega. Deposit ka unka share reconcile karenge. Furniture ka decide ho raha tha.

"Hum aa jayein?" Ma ne poocha.

"Abhi nahi."

"Akele kaise—"

"Main akela nahi hoon abhi."

Usne yeh bina soche kaha. Balcony ke glass mein kitchen dikh raha tha. Meera do plates nikaal rahi thi.

Papa ne poocha, "Khaya?"

"Abhi."

"Kha lo. Baad mein baat karenge."

Call band hua toh usse yaad aaya, Papa ne Meera ke baare mein koi bura sawal nahi poocha. Ho sakta tha Ma baad mein poochengi. Ho sakta tha Papa abhi shock mein hon. Usse nahi pata tha.

Wapas andar aaya toh phone par Kabir ka message tha: updated sheet?

Usne type kiya, ten minutes.

Meera ne plates rakh di.

Usne message delete kiya. Likha, tomorrow morning. Urgent changes call warehouse desk.

Send karte hi chest mein anxiety hui. Kahin actual escalation na aa jaye. Usne phone face down kar diya, phir do seconds baad ulta karke volume check kiya.

Meera ne dekha. Kuch nahi kaha.

"Bata diya?" usne poocha.

"Haan."

"Kya bole?"

"Rent poocha. Khana poocha."

Meera ne dal serve ki. "Meri Ma ne achar poocha tha. Pehle."

Woh thoda hansa. Meera nahi hansi, lekin uske mouth ke paas ki tightness kam hui.

Dal, roti aur fridge ka kal ka aloo. Aarav ne aloo liya. Meera ne mana kiya. Roti ka dabba uske paas tha. Usne bina pooche doosri roti Aarav ki plate mein rakh di, phir haath rok kar kaha, "Chahiye thi?"

"Haan."

Woh khane lage.

Beech mein usne kaha, "Naina ke yahan jaana ho toh jaa sakti ho. Mere liye yahan rehne ki zaroorat nahi."

Meera ne roti ka tukda plate mein rakha.

"Main tumhare liye nahi reh rahi."

"Mera matlab—"

"Pata hai. Main ek baar move karna chahti hoon. Kaam bhi hai. Yahan reh sakti hoon abhi. Agar uncomfortable hua toh chali jaoongi."

"Theek hai."

"Tumhe issue hai?"

"Nahi."

Usne sir hila diya. Pehli baar us shaam Aarav ko laga ki unhone ek baat theek se discuss ki hai. Chhoti thi. Isse woh galat importance nahi dena chahta tha.

Khane ke baad Meera ne mover ka estimate apne phone par khola.

"Thirteen August. Thursday. Eleven-thirty slot diya hai. Society office ko tum bol doge?"

"Haan."

"Kal bolna. Wednesday ko nahi."

Uski pehli reaction thi: mujhe yaad dilane ki zaroorat nahi. Lekin Wednesday ko bolne ki possibility real thi.

"Kal," usne kaha.

Sink mein plates rakhte hue usne count kiya. Aaj Thursday. Phir Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday.

Saat raat.

Usne calendar nahi khola. Phone par date already thi. Woh bas fridge ka door khol kar kal ke doodh ke liye jagah banane laga.

Meera ne peeche se poocha, "Kya dhoondh rahe ho?"

"Kuch nahi."

Haath mein uske ek khaali plastic dabba tha. Lid usse mil nahi rahi thi.
